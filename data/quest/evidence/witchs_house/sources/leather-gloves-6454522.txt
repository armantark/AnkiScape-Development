[[File:Leather gloves detail.png|left|100px|The leather gloves]]
'''Leather gloves''' are basic gloves worn on the hands that offer slight bonuses to [[Defence]]. Players can make these gloves through the [[Crafting]] skill at level 1 from [[leather|soft leather]], granting 13.75 Crafting [[experience]]. A [[account|player]] must have a [[needle]] and a [[thread]] to make them. They can also be found in the Varrock museum, on the wall of materials needed to clean the rocks.

Leather gloves can be fished with the [[big fishing net]] (giving no fishing experience). They also respawn south of the [[Edgeville]] bank and in [[Father Urhney]]'s hut in the [[Lumbridge Swamp]].

There used to be an infinite stock of them at [[Thessalia's Fine Clothes]]. Now there are only 10 in stock. Players who have completed the [[Digsite]] quest may obtain them from the tool rack in the [[Varrock Museum]] (but they will need to drop the gloves to obtain more than one).

The [[Gloves (Canifis)|Canifis gloves]] are coloured versions of these gloves.

Leather gloves are the only kind of [[food]] that [[Creeping hand]]s will eat.
