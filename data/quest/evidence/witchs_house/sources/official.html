Introduction

A young boy has kicked his ball into the garden of a scary old lady.

He asks you to get it back for him. This proves more difficult than it first sounds.


Quest Information

Inside the witch's garden

Members Only: Yes

Start Point: West of Falador.

To Start: Speak to the boy

Quest Difficulty: Intermediate

Quest Length: Medium

Quests Required:
  • None
Minimum Requirements:
  • Must be able to defeat a mystery level 53 monster
