# Creative pass rubric — translated and invented deeds, 2026-09-03

Owner rulings (queue §4.24, rulings 2 and 3): a translated or invented deed
shows no reference to its source deed, and it must be fun and creative with
difficulty similar to the source, not a lazy flattening.

Scope: the 125 `translated` and 37 `invented` rows. `active` and `dormant` rows
do not change.

## Each row must satisfy all of these

1. **Own text.** `designed_name` and `designed_description` are required. The
   description is one imperative sentence in the 2012 diary voice, 90
   characters or fewer. Neither field names anything the deed no longer
   contains (no fairy rings, no NPC talk, no "visit", no teleports).
2. **Region kept, places never instructed.** AnkiScape has no places. The
   instruction clause of the sentence names only things the player can select
   or do: a skill target, an item, an enemy, a shop, an activity. The region's
   story and its place names follow in a second clause that cannot be read as
   an instruction ("Catch five bass, the pride of Mudskipper Point."). The
   seam is the AnkiScape action closest to the source deed: the region's shop,
   enemy, quest, or skilling target where one is modelled. Amended 2026-09-04
   after the owner found "off Mudskipper Point" read as a location to select.
3. **Family kept.** The deed stays in the source's skill or activity family.
4. **Difficulty kept.** The level or count implied by the source deed carries
   over: easy tier is a low-level single action; medium a mid-level action or a
   small count; hard a high-level action or a long count; elite the top.
5. **Fun.** Names are puns or quips in the diary voice. The source name stays
   only when it still fits the new deed and references nothing removed.
6. **Unique seam.** No other row in the ledger may share the completion seam.
   Raise the count or choose the adjacent seam when the natural one is taken.
7. **Real wiring.** Every wiring key resolves to a real id in the registries.
   Copy the exact JSON shape of an existing row with the same `kind`.
8. **Record.** `notes` states the source deed, the new deed, and why the
   difficulty matches. `disposition` stays `translated` or `invented`.
