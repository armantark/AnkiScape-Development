## Finding

## Later `d2faf1d2` slice checks

- `quest-stage:eadgars_ruse:4` is not present on base `main` at `1cf43931`, so Troll thistle is not one of the 80 corrected items above. In `d2faf1d2`, stages 1–4 replace their inert required actions with runtime `auto_actions`; stage 4 still requires real inventory and then grants `Troll thistle`. The test helper supplies inventory and levels but does not seed `quest_stage_actions`. Troll thistle, Dried thistle, and Ground thistle therefore remain genuinely stage-reachable in that commit.

- The same commit added unconditional quest-combat guaranteed-drop harvesting. That was a second false-reachability path. The corrected tool now applies the stage-action gate to combat drops and also requires runtime combat stats plus a minimum combat level no higher than the maximum normal player combat level.

- Nial Swiftfling defaults `minimum_player_combat` to enemy level 156. A normal maxed player is combat level 126, so the real kernel returns `combat_underpowered`; the regression helper only passes by assigning level 400 combat stats. `Herb pouch`, `Diamond root`, and `Diamond root dust` are therefore inert in normal play in `d2faf1d2`, despite its audit claiming them reachable. No route was repaired here.

## Scope

This change fixes and tests the audit only. It does not add quest action emitters, change quest catalogs, lower combat gates, grant items, or repair any inert route. Each recorded route is a candidate for a later content slice.
