# Class rulings for the Sol loop questions of 2026-11-04

Date: `2026-11-04`

These rulings continue the numbering of `grill-decisions-2026-10-28.md`. Each settles a class of contract questions. ## 19. Item and reward values where period sources disagree

Ruling line: ruling 7 also covers items and rewards. The contract takes the more specific dated item article and does not ask the owner. A saving effect on several runes rolls once per rune.

## 20. Level to buy and level to use

Ruling line: all listed levels are required both to buy and to use a reward. When only one source lists a level, that level applies only to equip the item.

## 21. Prayers that a scroll teaches

Ruling line: every save needs the scroll, including saves that already selected the prayer. Saved prayer settings stay and return once the prayer is learned. A scroll already read or held unread cannot be bought or read again.

## 22. Missing art and small details

Ruling line: ship the playable content now. Use a verified near picture marked as a Placeholder where one exists, otherwise text, and record the gap. Art must eventually come into place, in this order: an authenticated period picture, then the matching OSRS or RS3 picture when nothing genuine exists. This is an art-only exception to the boundary against modern substitution. It replaces the "keep the current one" outcome of ruling 18 as a final state.

## 23. Bone necklaces

Ruling line: each necklace restores Prayer for the bones its own article names, at the same amount for manual burial, automatic burial and Bonecrusher.
