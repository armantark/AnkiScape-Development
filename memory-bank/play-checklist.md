# Things to try while you study

Each line is one change that is in your game and that automatic tests already
pass, but that no person has seen in play yet. Try a line when your reviews
take you there anyway; no line needs a special session.

- Works: delete the line.
- Fails or looks wrong: tell the orchestrator what you saw; it becomes a fix.

Slices that change what the player sees add their line here, grouped by where
you are in the game, with the date the change reached main.

## Any skill

- [ ] A big XP lamp or a quest that gives many levels shows one level-up window from the old level to the new one. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Put on or take off gear right after a review, then press Undo: the gear change goes back first, and the item is not both worn and in the bank. (2026-09-28)

## Combat

- [ ] Pick a monster: its max hit and hit chance include the Strength, Attack or Ranged prayer you will have up in that fight. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] With Prayer set to Off, your carried Prayer potions stay full. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] After a quest fight, an ordinary fight still pulls you out when you run low with no food. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] With a bow and the right supplies, a manual Ranged finishing blow goes through. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Above your max life points from a Tiger shark, eat a kebab or a beer: the extra life points stay. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] A summoned Bunyip does not heal you inside Daemonheim, and heals you again once you leave. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] A death in the red Clan Wars portal costs everything except the one item Protect Item saves. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Stronghold of Player Safety: from another skill, press "Fight Cockroach soldier"; the next review fights the soldier on Combat. Undo returns you to your skill. (2026-09-29)

## Minigames

- [ ] Castle Wars, Pest Control, Soul Wars and Barbarian Assault fights give their XP once per fight, not twice. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Barbarian Advanced course: a failed log costs that lap's bonus XP, and the next clean lap earns it again. (2026-09-28)

## Gathering and processing skills

- [ ] Runecrafting: Astral runes open after Lunar Diplomacy with level 40 and Pure essence; no Astral talisman is asked for. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Runecrafting: combination runes use only what is in your pack, and Magic Imbue still saves the talisman. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Runecrafting: a Wicked hood teleport to the altar gives your Summoning points back, as a walk there does. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Cooking: with the fish's Gauntlets setting on Casual, you keep your Brawling gloves when Cooking gauntlets would not stop a burn. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Fletching headless arrows answers quickly, also with a very large bank. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Auto-drink removes only the dose you drank from the carried pack, not an extra bottle. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Thieving: a failed attempt keeps its damage and stun when your pack yak banks an item, and the next review clears the stun. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] Thieving: fail a blackjack knockout with "Fight back when caught" on; you fight the Menaphite Thug with the food, arrows and runes you carry. (2026-09-28)

## Bank, shops and items

- [ ] A half-full urn in a grave or Death's Office keeps its progress across a reload, and fills again once you carry it. (2026-09-28)
- [ ] A sold-out shop item comes back on schedule, also when you keep buying other things in that shop. (2026-09-28)
