# Things to try while you study

Each line is one change that is in your game and that automatic tests already
pass, but that no person has seen in play yet. Try a line when your reviews
take you there anyway; no line needs a special session.

- Works: delete the line.
- Fails or looks wrong: tell the orchestrator what you saw; it becomes a fix.

Slices that change what the player sees add their line here, grouped by where
you are in the game, with the date the change reached main.
