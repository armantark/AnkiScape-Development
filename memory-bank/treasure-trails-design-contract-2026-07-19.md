# Treasure Trails design contract

Date: `2026-07-19`

Cutoff: RuneScape immediately before EOC, `2012-11-20`.

Status: **owner-settled model (one step per review, typed flattening); all
step pools, requirements, and reward tables are harvest work.**

## Owner-settled model (2026-07-19)

- A clue scroll is a persistent inventory item; the authentic one-clue-per-
  tier ownership rule holds across easy, medium, hard, and Elite (2010).
- Each successful review resolves the clue's current step with
  **type-preserving flattening**:
  - map and coordinate steps become one abstracted travel-and-dig beat;
  - emote steps become a gear-check beat — the required equipment must
    actually be owned and equipped, so the real acquisition gate survives
    while the emote animation (excluded system) does not;
  - riddle and challenge-NPC steps resolve via their source answers;
  - puzzle boxes collapse into the completing beat, per the settled
    Barrows-door precedent (no puzzle UI, no invented failure roll).
- Step counts per clue use the authentic tier-scaled rolls.
- The casket opens as a direct command with sourced tier reward tables (god
  pages, trimmed armour, Third Age, and the rest), atomic and undoable.

## Harvest work

Pin per tier from the period record: acquisition sources and rates, step
pools and their gear/quest requirements, step-count distributions, casket
reward tables and roll structure, and the Elite scroll-box/puzzle additions.
The unpinned reward rolls follow the verification standard's provisional
rules where the period record is silent.
