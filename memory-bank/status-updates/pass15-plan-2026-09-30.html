Five rulings are the orchestrator's choices. You can reverse each one.

You did not do a grill for Pass 15. So the orchestrator made these five choices from your notes, the 2012 sources and the rules that the game already obeys.

R1 · S4

Quest fights last several reviews

WHY
A quest fight then plays like any other fight, and you see the boss take damage.
IF YOU REVERSE IT
A quest fight ends in one step again, as it does now.
R2 · S4

Delrith needs a wielded Silverlight

WHY
The game says “You need silverlight to damage Delrith.”
IF YOU REVERSE IT
A Silverlight in the bank counts again.
R3 · S5

Barcrawl bars in a fixed order

WHY
The stages use the order that the blocked-quest dialog shows now, A to Z by bar name.
IF YOU REVERSE IT
Name another order, for example the order in which you walk to the bars.
R4 · S6

Belt tools count without ownership

WHY
Skills already work this way. Your note 6 says that the tools can be on the belt by default.
IF YOU REVERSE IT
A tool counts only after you own one or add it to the belt.
R5 · S7

Locked shops go last but still open

WHY
You can look at the stock and the prices before you unlock the shop.
IF YOU REVERSE IT
A locked shop does not open, or does not show at all.
