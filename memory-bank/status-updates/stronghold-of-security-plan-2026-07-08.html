Stronghold of Security

A one-time, no-combat activity that walks the player through the four dungeons of the Stronghold and pays out on completion: 10,000 coins plus a one-time choice between Fancy boots and Fighting boots. Deliberately kept out of the parity-locked quest catalog — no quest points, no journal entry. This is the last item on the combat-overhaul list; nothing here is built yet.

Parity flavor — the four horsemen

Real OSRS structure maps cleanly onto the four steps. In-game the Stronghold is four dungeons named for the horsemen of the apocalypse, and the two rewards you named come from two of them:

Step 1 · War
Vault of War
Entry level. Safety questions, no reward.
Step 2 · Famine
Catacomb of Famine
In RS: grants the boots choice.
Step 3 · Pestilence
Pit of Pestilence
Deeper level, no reward.
Step 4 · Death
Sepulchre of Death
In RS: grants the 10,000 coins.
Simplification: RS hands the boots out after level 2 and the coins after level 4. Since AnkiScape has no in-dungeon dialogue trees, the proposal pays both rewards together on completing step 4. The four names are cosmetic labels for the four steps — the money and boots stay the same. Flip the flavor off in Decision D4 if you'd rather see plain "Step 1–4".

Proposed model

Where it lives. The Utility / Activities hub already renders a grouped tree of no-XP activities and advances the selected one per review (current_utility → apply_utility_activity_pure). The Stronghold slots in there as a distinct one-time card under a new landmarks group, so it sits beside the other activities without pretending to be a repeatable material conversion.

State. One new nested key on player_data:

player_data["stronghold_of_security"] = {"step": 0, "completed": False, "boots_choice": None}

Advance. While the Stronghold card is the active activity, each review advances step by one (0→1→2→3→4). A new pure helper — apply_stronghold_step_pure(player_data) — owns this, mirroring apply_utility_activity_pure's "return a new copy, don't mutate" contract. No XP, no items along the way.

Completion. When step reaches 4, the helper flips completed=True, and the UI surfaces a one-time boot-choice dialog. Choosing grants the reward through the existing inventory-add pattern:

inventory["Coins"] += 10000 · inventory[chosen_boots] += 1 · stronghold["boots_choice"] = chosen
+10,000 Coins and one of:
Fancy bootsfeet · def +1/+2/+3, magic −3, ranged −1item id 9005 · appearance 1810
Fighting bootsfeet · identical bonusesitem id 9006 · appearance 1811

Idempotency. Once completed is true, the card renders "Completed ✓", stops advancing, and the reward can never be granted twice (guarded on boots_choice is None, the same one-shot pattern as a claims[...]["claimed"] flag).

New equipment. Two EquipmentItem entries in equipment_data.py, both slot="feet", required_level=1, carrying the exact items.yml bonuses (defence_stab +1, defence_slash +2, defence_crush +3, attack_magic −3, attack_ranged −1; all else 0), attack_speed=4, source citing items.yml 9005 / 9006 and the Stronghold of Security. The two are stat-identical, so the choice is cosmetic — no invented zeros, per parity-over-safeguards.

  3. UI card + choice dialog. medium The one-time card in the Utility hub (progress "Step n / 4", "Completed ✓"), wired to advance on review, plus the boot-choice dialog on completion. Offscreen render check per the Qt screenshot rule.

Test plan

  • Unit: apply_stronghold_step_pure advances 0→4, is a no-op at 4, never mutates input.
  • Unit: completion grants exactly +10,000 Coins and exactly one chosen boot; a second call grants nothing.
  • Unit: both boots exist as feet-slot equipment with the exact items.yml bonuses and resolve through the equip path.
  • Integration: selecting the card as current_utility advances it on a review tick.
  • Offscreen render: card shows step progress and the "Completed ✓" state in dark mode.
  • Manual: run the four reviews, pick each boot on a fresh profile, confirm coins + boot land and the card locks.
