The cleanup is judged by signal, not by making the number smaller. A test stays when it owns a distinct behavior, boundary, historical contract, migration, or UI interaction. It goes when it is dead, text-only, duplicated without a new branch, or present mainly to make the suite look larger.

Tests that never prove behavior

  • Delete undiscovered pytest-style functions from the unittest suite.

Consolidate

One behavior, one test, many scenarios

Constant-only copies become table-driven subtests when setup, mutation, and assertions are the same. Distinct branches remain distinct methods even when their shapes happen to resemble one another.

Protect

Coverage that earns its maintenance cost

  • Pure behavior, state transitions, migrations, undo, and failure atomicity.
  • Runtime integration and real offscreen Qt widget interaction.
  • Source-backed catalog and asset integrity locks.
  • Expected-failure specifications for confirmed unresolved defects.

Definition of done

Every test-shaped function is either discovered or explicitly marked as a helper.
No test proves behavior by reading production source text.
No exact duplicate test bodies remain.
Every consolidated scenario remains visible as a named subtest.
Known product defects remain pinned, not deleted to make output green.
Pure and real offscreen Qt suites retain their prior behavioral coverage.

Count is deliberately not a success metric. The final number may fall modestly or substantially; the proof is that named scenarios and meaningful boundaries remain while mechanically detectable slop reaches zero.
