Why Summoning is next

Dungeoneering depends on a wider finished skill surface and a temporary economy. Summoning is bounded, reuses live Combat/inventory/death/quest systems, and de-risks both Slayer charm acquisition and future Daemonheim variants.

Ordinary familiar state will never leak into Daemonheim. That boundary stays owned by the later Dungeoneering contract.

What “complete” means

  • All period pouches, familiars, scrolls, ingredients, points, obelisks, and acquisition routes.
  • Combat, healing, BoB, foraging, boosting, teleport, skilling, and bespoke abilities.
  • Searchable UI, real locks, assets, persistence, item conservation, death, and both undo paths.
  • Source-blocked hidden rolls remain visible and may receive only labeled, bounded abstractions.

The source already closes one tempting shortcut

Duration and points are separate. Every familiar had its own minute duration and summon cost; points kept draining during its life.
Zero points did not dismiss it. The familiar remained, but point-gated right-click abilities stopped until restoration.
Scroll specials were separate again. They did not consume ordinary Summoning points.

So AnkiScape must retain the authentic two-layer lifecycle even if the controls are flattened.

Decision 01

What spends a familiar’s lifetime?

Recommended

Every answered card

Closest discrete analogue to global uptime. Preserves long-duration value across target switches and cannot be frozen by choosing an ineligible action.

Successful gameplay actions

Less punishing during unrelated study, but converts duration into usage time and lets the player preserve familiars indefinitely.

Session boundary only

Simplest presentation, but largely erases source duration tiers and weakens renewal and pouch cost.

Recommendation: one global minutes-to-review-ticks conversion, preserving every familiar’s exact relative duration. The magnitude is explicitly invented for AnkiScape; no wall clock is read.
