What Changed

Backend

combat_data.py now carries the standard elemental spell rows through Fire Wave, using the source spellbook metadata instead of a level-only approximation.

Resource Readiness

Higher-tier rune requirements flow through the existing staff substitution and missing-supply checks, including Blood rune requirements for Wave spells.

Frontend

The existing spell combo exposes the higher spell ids; tests cover selecting Wind Bolt, Fire Blast, and Fire Wave from the UI-facing control path.

Deferred Intentionally

God spells, Ancient spells, Lunar spells, and special-effect combat spells remain out until their unlock, staff, quest, and effect semantics can be represented honestly.
