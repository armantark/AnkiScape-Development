Pre-Implementation Grill

Runecrafting has one real product decision before code.

The source table is clear enough to implement. The design hinge is what one successful Anki review means when the original altar action converts all carried essence but AnkiScape stores bank-like item counts.

Source Shape

The local 2011Scape rev 667 plugin is the source of truth. It exposes standard altar crafting, combination runes, and a small active tiara subset. Pouches exist in source but are transport/storage mechanics, not the target row itself.
