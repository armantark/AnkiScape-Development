Iron Smelting RNG Correction

Backend slice to align AnkiScape Smithing with the local 2011Scape source: iron bars can fail after consuming ore, while other furnace bars remain deterministic.

Source chance
50%-80%

Iron bar success interpolates from Smithing level 15 to 45 in the local emulator.

Other smelting RNG
None

Bronze, blurite, silver, steel, gold, mithril, adamant, and rune do not have a smelt-fail roll in the audited source.

Current add-on
100%

The existing runtime treats iron like every other Smithing recipe, so it never loses impure ore.
