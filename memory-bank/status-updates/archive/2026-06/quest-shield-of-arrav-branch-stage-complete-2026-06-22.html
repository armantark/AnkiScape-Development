Completed slice, not completed goal

Shield of Arrav is now branch-aware.

Backend and frontend work are split cleanly: the data/runtime layer now models Phoenix and Black Arm as route variants, and the Quest UI proves started quests show those route blockers. The all-quest parity goal remains active because 108 combat rows are still statless and broad per-stage extraction is not minute-detail complete.
