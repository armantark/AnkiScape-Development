COMPLETED / HUNTER SUPPLY BRIDGE

Hunter gear now has a narrow acquisition path

The final shop interface is still deferred. This pass adds the smallest live bridge: spend quest-scale coins once, receive source-backed Hunter supplies, and automatically switch off after the review.

Design decision

Tools are gates, not consumables

Hunter targets now split reusable gear from items that should disappear. Snares, nets, and box traps stay in inventory as capability checks; jars, logs, and magic boxes are still consumed only on successful catches.

Why this is not the final shop
