Backend Contract

Stage Model

Stage 8 is the completion marker, so the final playable gate lives on stage 7. This avoids unreachable final-stage requirements in the existing quest progression engine.

Items

Bucket, Bucket of wax, Black candle, Bread, Bat bones, and Lit black candle now move at their source stages. Excalibur is produced during the quest and retained.

Combat

Sir Mordred is a resolver-ready Darkan-stat combat gate. Thrantax is a ritual dialogue/action gate, not invented combat.

Merlin Stage Path

1Round-table investigation with Gawain, Lucan, and Lancelot.
2Catherby crate route into Keep Le Faye.
3Sir Mordred combat, gated by global battle config and player stats.
4Morgan questioning, Bucket plus Insect repellent, Bucket of wax output.
5Bucket of wax becomes Black candle.
6Bread is handed to the beggar and Excalibur is produced.
7Bat bones, Black candle, Tinderbox, Excalibur, ritual, crystal smash, Arthur report.
8Completion marker: 6 quest points, no duplicate Excalibur reward.
