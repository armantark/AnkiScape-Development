Why pass 3
The type-based smithing selector maps armour and weapon families but skips smithable tools that are also wieldable. Bronze/Iron/Steel hatchet slipped in via drops; the higher tiers nothing drops did not. Merging a recipe-based smithing source last adds exactly those three names without relabelling any of the 550 existing rows.

The one deliberate exclusion

They stay out by design, parked with the minigames-activities-design-seed.md. The same is true for metal boots above Rune that the current roster does not yet drop.
