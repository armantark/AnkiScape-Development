PRE-IMPLEMENTATION PLAN · 2026-07-06

Shop-Economy Integration Pass

NPC shops shipped with 2,695 live priced rows, but the rest of the game still acts like they don't exist: free "temporary bridge" activities duplicate shop stock, nobody has verified a player can actually earn the coins the shops charge, and recipes that were parked as input-starved now have purchasable inputs. Three stages, in order, each committed separately.

STAGE 1 · RETIRE SHOP-COVERED BRIDGES

Remove the three activities whose own source notes say "temporary until shops"

  • scavenge_chicken_feathers — note says "temporary scavenging bridge until Combat/GE". Feathers are now live in 13 shops at 6 Coins, and the Chicken combat target drops them.
  • gather_fishing_bait — note says "temporary no-XP bridge" citing Lumbridge Fishing Supplies stock. Fishing bait is live at 3 Coins in the fishing shops.
  • buy_hunter_supplies — note says "until shops/GE get a full UI". All 11 bundle items are individually live (Aleck's Hunter Emporium and friends), at real cache prices instead of the flat bundle price.

Storage config bumps 25 → 26: saves whose current_utility points at a removed id migrate to the default activity instead of erroring on the next review. Item registrations stay (Feather, bait, and Hunter gear now resolve as shop stock); orphaned activity icons are removed from the manifest. Tests updated for the removed rows plus a migration test.

STAGE 2 · CLOSE THE COIN LOOP

Prove gather → sell → buy works end to end, and write down the income map

Spending exists (267 coin shops, services); income is combat Coins drops, general-store selling, and the bartender glass sale — but no one has verified the loop end to end. Deliverables:

  • A source-audit doc mapping every coin income route with its data source, plus a data check that the primary gathering outputs (logs, ores, fish, gems, hides) are tradeable and sellable at a general store.
  • An integration test: a player with gathered goods sells to a general store via apply_shop_sell_pure, gains coins, buys Feathers with them, and the whole sequence stays undo-compatible through the existing shop event rollback.
  • Any key-mismatch or sell-policy bug the audit surfaces gets fixed at the backend layer, not papered over in Qt.

The one real gap is Molten glass: glassblowing products exist but nothing produces their input. The 2011Scape furnace source (furnaces.plugin.kts · handleMoltenGlass) defines it exactly: 1 Soda ash + 1 Bucket of sand → Molten glass plus the Bucket back, 20 Crafting XP, no level gate. That needs a small optional byproducts field on Crafting recipes (the schema is single-output today), the recipe row, a Molten glass icon with provenance, and tests. Recipe notes that still say "input-starved until molten glass source exists" get refreshed, and the kanban's NPC-shop and parked-Crafting cards get their statuses corrected.
