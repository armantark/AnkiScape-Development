Settled design

Combat overlay

EV prayers, no flicking

Protection/deflect multiply each enemy hit by (1 − uptime) at logic_pure.py:3502; offensive prayers are pre-loop effective-level multipliers at :3457; drain accrues per simulated tick at authentic rate × uptime in the loop at :3435. Checkbox loadout per combat target, authentic conflict groups enforced.

Prayer points

Points, drain resistance, restores

Max = prayer level (internally level×10). Resistance = 60 + 2 × prayer equipment bonus (game-main prayer.kt; the equipment_data.py prayer field already exists). Bank-run refill is free; prayer potions auto-drink mid-burst; prayers switch off at 0 points.

Ancient curses

Full curse book, sourced EV ramp

Unlock: The Temple at Senntisten; global book toggle at burst boundaries. Sap procs 25%/attack, leech 20%/attack (Darkan CurseManager) ramping to the pinned caps (oldid 6535713). Deflect reflects 10% × 60% = 6% EV of raw hits. Turmoil scales off enemy base levels; Soul Split heals 20% of damage dealt. NPC-meaningless curses ship included-but-inert.

Non-combat analogs

Rapid Restore family

Baseline recovery is the existing 100-tick cadence (logic_pure.py:2529); Rapid Restore multiplies the step by (1 + uptime), excluding Constitution/Prayer/Summoning (oldid 6268755). Rapid Heal scales the LP trickle; Berserker stretches boost decay ×(1 + 0.15·uptime).
