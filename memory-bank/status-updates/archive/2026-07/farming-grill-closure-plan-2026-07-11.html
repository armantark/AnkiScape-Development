01Settled decisions OWNER POLICY PIN-GATED

Review-ticked clock OWNER

240 answered cards = 24 hours (6 era-minutes per card). Nothing reads wall-clock time. Failures tick the clock too — the first all-answers counter, because it models time, not skill. Era stage minutes stay honest in data; ticks process eagerly per card so Anki undo restores exact prior state, rolls included.

Queue preempts the active skill OWNER

N queued steps → the next N successful reviews execute farm steps instead of the active skill's action, with a visible indicator, then control returns. "Harvest & replant same" is a first-class per-patch shortcut; first use walks through plan setup.

Full Nov-2012 patch map OWNER PIN

Every era patch, location-modeled and gated by level/quest (diary gating checked at pin time). Flower→allotment protection falls out naturally: Falador marigolds protect Falador allotments. Habitat patches ship live but emergently inert until Jadinko hunting supplies seeds. Jade vine & evil turnip ship only if pins prove complete loops.

Full-parity disease OWNER

GM model exactly: floor(slots × compost × 0.9ʷ)/128 per stage; compost 0.28, super 0.14; diseased crop dies at its next stage boundary. Crops can die unattended — the HUD badge is the counterweight. Disease is pure era RNG, never coupled to answer correctness.

No auto-suggestions OWNER

"Best at my level" rejected — too automatic, and players farm specific ingredients. "Queue full run" executes only the player's own per-patch plans: harvest ready, cure diseased, clear dead, replant-same where a plan exists; unplanned patches are skipped.

Modifiers by precedent POLICY PIN

Magic secateurs = bank-ownership (crystal-saw pattern) behind Fairytale I, ×1.1 save slots. Juju potion ships wired but marble-vine-starved. Greenfingers aura (loyalty meta) & scroll of life (Dungeoneering) defer; patch bomb struck if post-cutoff. Farming/nature amulets: superseded by the HUD badge, function-trailed. Cure Plant & Fertile Soil gain real function per Magic decision 9.
