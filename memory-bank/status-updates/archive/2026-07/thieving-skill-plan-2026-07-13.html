Explicit workstreams

Backend and frontend stay separate in implementation and verification, even though they land as one coherent skill.

Backend

Source-shaped mechanics

  • Stable target data for pickpockets, stalls, wall safe, and source-closed blackjack rows.
  • Interpolation against 255, multi-pickpocket, loot, damage, stun, shared LP, and food substrate.
  • Exact rational stall residuals through the shipped cadence kernel.
  • Additive storage migration, action dispatch, achievements, quest gates, and Phoenix-feather Utility route.
Frontend

Ordinary Skills-hub experience

  • Thieving graduates from planned to complete with grouped target rows.
  • Level/quest/item locks are normal disabled rows; no special “input-starved” UI.
  • Three casual controls total, including “Fight back when caught”; advanced editors own deeper control.
  • Stats, Bank, HUD, failure feedback, and real offscreen Qt coverage.
Assets

First-class visual identity

  • Detail-resolution Thieving skill icon.
  • Visible NPC/stall/loot/blackjack/activity icons with recorded provenance.
  • Period cache or verified RuneScape Wiki art preferred; no silent OSRS substitution.
  • Manifest/path existence tests and text-only fallback only for genuinely unresolved single icons.
Balance & evidence

No free parameters

  • Source XP remains untouched; cadence corrects rates.
  • Stall catch chance stays zero; wall safe keeps its only explicit failure roll.
  • Level inversion ships honestly; no world-hop abstraction.
  • 2011Scape silence parks a row as source_blocked, even when Darkan has a lead.

Non-negotiable mechanics

These are acceptance contracts, not implementation suggestions.

pickpocket
Linear endpoint interpolation across levels 1–99, rolled against the single fixed ceiling 255; failures consume the review, damage shared LP, and set a one-review stun-clearing state.
stall cadence
rate_limited_profile_pure("thieving:" + stall_id, 6000, respawn_ticks); vegetable, baker, and wall safe explicitly declare cap_one_action.
stall failure
Ordinary stalls have no invented catch roll. Positional guards are omitted because AnkiScape has no location layer. Wall safe alone uses endpoints 8→160 against 255.
failure stake
The next review clears Thieving stun and performs no action. “Fight back when caught” is one shared toggle for caught-stall and blackjack-thug handling.
evidence
Every shipped number lands in the Thieving tables audit with actual source file:line or pinned wiki oldid; dossier shorthand never supplies data.

Source gate

The brief asks blackjacking and The Feud to ship, while the implementation contract also says 2011Scape-silent rows cannot inherit Darkan values. The extraction pass must prove every required blackjack value from 2011Scape or a pinned period wiki revision. Any unproved concrete value parks that row rather than being guessed; a true contradiction in the settled contract stops implementation for a direct report.
