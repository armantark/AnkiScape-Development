What changed

ASSETS · GENERATOR-DERIVED BACKFILL

570-item manifest, 547 sprites fetched

New tools/fetch_shop_assets.py derives its manifest from SHOP_DATA (visible live/locked stock with no registry asset anywhere) and fetched through the shared rate-limited wiki client. SHOP_ITEM_IMAGES existence-scans the folder, so the new art wired itself into the item registry, and shop selector icons (first stock icon) filled in for free. A new registry test pins the surviving gap list so future generator growth can't silently ship iconless stock.

Unresolvable (color-variant-only wiki files): Boots, Hat, Shirt, Skirt, Shorts, Trousers, Gloves, Robe top/bottoms, Fremennik cloak/shirt, pirate + villager + tribal clothing, Shantay pass, Damaged book · Registry identity collision: Willow-blackjack (backend follow-up flagged)
tools/fetch_shop_assets.pyshopitems/assets_provenance.jsontests/test_skill_and_item_registry.py
ICONS · NEAREST-NEIGHBOR SCALING

Uniform crisp sprites at every call site

Wiki sprites vary from ~12px to ~40px and QIcon never upscales, which produced the tiny-vs-normal mix. New _pixel_item_icon scales every sprite to its slot (32px tiles, 24px lists) with FastTransformation — nearest neighbor, no blur — sized for the screen's device pixel ratio and cached. Stock tiles, the shop selector, search rows, sell rows, and service rows all route through it.

NAVIGATION · BROWSE-FIRST

No default shop; click to open; Close ✕ collapses

The tab opens to a full-height shop list (~19 shops visible at once) with a hint line. Clicking a shop compacts the list and opens the detail panel; the Close ✕ button collapses it and clears the selection. Search results no longer auto-select either — nothing opens until you choose it. The old auto-selected default (and its accidental setCurrentRow(0) path) is gone.
