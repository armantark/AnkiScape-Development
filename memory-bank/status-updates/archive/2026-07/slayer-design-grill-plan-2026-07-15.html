Known knowns

Branches the grill will not reopen

Settled

Fights stay in Combat

Slayer does not duplicate accuracy, attack ticks, supplies, drops, LP, death, graves, or target selection.

Settled

Progress comes from kills

Combat damage still grants ordinary combat XP; qualifying kills advance the assignment and grant the sourced Slayer XP.

Settled

One review remains atomic

A review may advance a combat burst, but Slayer progress only commits with the same kill transaction and rolls back with Anki undo.

Settled

No map and no wall clock

Master access and monster contexts become explicit gates or target-local context—not global position or elapsed-time state.

Question 1 · surface handoff

After a player accepts an assignment, how much should Slayer choose for them?

Assignments often name a family, while individual variants differ in risk, drops, access gates, and equipment. The choice determines whether Slayer feels like an intentional contract layered over Combat or an opaque auto-battler.

Recommended
Open filtered Combat choices

Record the task, then show only qualifying variants in Combat's Slayer filter. The player deliberately chooses the exact monster before reviews begin.

Alternative
Auto-select the safest variant

Fast, but hides meaningful loot, danger, access, and equipment differences behind an implicit decision.

Alternative
Leave Combat untouched

The assignment persists, but the player must manually search all enemies and infer which ones count.

COMPLETENESS RULEMissing evidence may block or label an exact value; it may not erase a period feature. Missing enemy or activity owners become explicit dependencies with stable integration boundaries.
