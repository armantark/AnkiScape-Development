Stronghold of Security — Built

The one-time landmark shipped exactly as planned: four no-combat steps that advance one per review, a completion payout of 10,000 coins plus a cosmetic boot choice, and two new stat-identical feet-slot items. It lives in the Utility hub under a new Landmarks group, entirely outside the parity-locked quest catalog. This was the final item on the combat-overhaul list.

What shipped, against the plan

🏛️
D1 · Utility hub, new "Landmarks" group as planned
Injected by hand into _build_utility_list as a distinct top-level group, since a step counter isn't a repeatable UTILITY_ACTIVITY_DATA conversion. It renders beside the other activities without pretending to be one.
4️⃣
D2 · One review per step, 4 total as planned
Each review while the card is active calls apply_stronghold_step_pure once, advancing 0→1→2→3→4. No XP or items en route.
👢
D3 · Boot-choice dialog at completion as planned
Clearing step 4 fires a deferred (QTimer.singleShot) modal so it never pops mid-answer. The same dialog is reachable from the card if the player dismisses it — the row shows "Claim your reward ✨" until claimed.
🐎
D4 · Four horsemen step names as planned
Vault of War → Catacomb of Famine → Pit of Pestilence → Sepulchre of Death, surfaced in the per-step "Cleared the …" toast.
