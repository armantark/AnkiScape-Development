The nine owner decisions

settled

Alchemy: full parity, uncapped

Low/High Alch on any tradeable item at authentic Darkan-cache values. No faucet safeguard — the owner explicitly doesn't care. Fire-staff ownership waives fire runes on noncombat casts too (best-owned-tool convention). UI is a bank item picker.

settled

Teleports: pure training casts

Full solo-teleport roster as XP cast targets — Camelot spam was a real era method. No invented travel hooks; travel is already fully abstracted everywhere.

settled

All three spellbooks, one slice

Standard completion + Ancients (Desert Treasure) + Lunar (Lunar Diplomacy). Finally gives astral, cosmic, nature, law, and blood runes their consumers — rune supply was already solved by RC altars + live shops.

settled

Implicit book + strict no-mixing

No spellbook toggle exists; each spell implies its book, quest gates are the only locks. But one go (preset or stint) may not mix books — pure logic derives the book from all selected effects and rejects mixes. Veng + Ancients autocast: illegal.

exception

Spellbook Swap: the one exception

Lunar 96 authentically allows exactly one off-book cast. It ships functional: a one-book preset/stint may include a single off-book spell, paying the swap runes per fight/stint. The level-96 capstone actually means something.

new mechanic

Dream: heal with reviews, not food

Between fights, missing HP can heal via Dream casts at a review cost derived from its authentic heal rate. Time-for-supplies is exactly the real spell's trade; reviews are our time.

ux

Casual mode: dial + 3 toggles

The pack-mode dial derives prayers, uptime, Vengeance, and the swap slot from unlocks. Casual surfaces exactly three toggles (below). Full editor in advanced. Owner: "we'll probably have to tweak it" — keep the toggle set data-driven.

deferred

Livid Farm spells wait

Vengeance Group, Disruption Shield, etc. unlock through a grindy points minigame — a real activity family needing its own grill. Deferred to its card; the Lunar book is otherwise complete.

settled

Farming handoff note

Cure Plant / Fertile Soil ship as pure casts now, but the Farming card carries an owner-mandated note: they must gain real function (a farming-run option) when Farming lands — not stay ceremonial.
