What the model guarantees

The good version of a percentage grant.

  • The denominator is frozen per ledger page.
  • Previously earned rewards never move backward.
  • Offers prefer unowned families before upgrades.
  • “Surprise me” can exist without forcing randomness.
Main risk: the achievement that fills the meter may have nothing to do with the item offered.
Best at

Giving the whole catalog a simple, legible reward cadence.

Worst failure

Diary gear becomes generic account loot rather than the result of related mastery.

Build burden · Low–medium

Frozen ledgers, offer eligibility, migration, and a claim surface.

Current synthesis

Use immutable item-family tracks as the base. Let each tier accept a curated subset of requirements, and reserve signature deeds for tiers where a genuinely memorable capstone exists. A global percentage bar can award a wildcard Diary Writ, but should not independently gate the item track or force random gear.
