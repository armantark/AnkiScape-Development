The four decisions — how they landed

How much detail on the always-on HUD?
As recommended: two bars + XP chips on the HUD; the full per-skill XP bars live on the Combat stats screen.
Auto-flee: default and trigger?
As recommended: ON by default, triggers when LP is at or below the auto-eat threshold and no food remains.
Safe-recovery rate — parity call?
Deviated from the recommendation. The plan's pick was "keep +100/review, just relabel it." You chose parity instead: heal to full over 3 rest reviews, then a slow ~5 LP/review natural trickle.
Consistent with the standing parity-over-safeguards rule — recorded so the plan-vs-actual gap is intentional, not an accident.
Build order?
As recommended: staged, one commit per stage (four stages below).
