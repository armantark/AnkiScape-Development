00 · TARGET

Make Farming AnkiScape’s first pure background skill: every answer moves the world clock, successful answers may detour into one queued field verb, and the farm surface remains compact enough to operate without becoming a global checkbox wall.

04 · STATE

Disease and growth boundary model

The closed contract ships the internally sound GM stage formula and labels its exact factors as GM-sourced where the historical pages are only qualitative:

floor(diseaseSlots × compostFactor × (0.9 if watered)) / 128

Factors: none 1.00, compost 0.28, supercompost 0.14. Compost adds +1 harvest life; supercompost +2. First stage, paid/protected, same-complex flower or scarecrow protection, and immune crops skip the ordinary roll. A crop still diseased at its next boundary dies.

Compute all boundaries crossed by the new global clock, ordered by time.
At each boundary, resolve an existing disease into death before considering new growth risk.
Apply immunity, farmer, flower, scarecrow, watering, and compost state; disease RNG never reads answer correctness.
Advance stage, ready state, produce regrowth, compost maturation, and alert counts.
Persist the complete result, including RNG outcomes, before review dispatch returns.
