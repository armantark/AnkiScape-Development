Two bars (you + enemy), XP chips lit for the skills your style actually trains, a kill tally, and the menu button anchored to the right of the bar. Full per-skill XP progress bars live on the Combat stats screen, matching every other skill.

Chips are indicative. Accurate lights ATK+HP, Aggressive STR+HP, Defensive DEF+HP, Controlled splits ATK/STR/DEF+HP — so the HUD itself answers "where is my XP going?" without opening a menu.

The eleven asks & how each is met

1
Chain kills Mechanics Med
A 50-tick (~30s) burst should keep killing weak enemies until the tick budget is spent, instead of stopping after the first kill and wasting half the window.
Replace the break at logic_pure.py:3200 with a respawn-and-continue loop: on kill, tally it, roll drops, reset enemy LP to max, keep consuming ticks. Return kills + aggregated drops/xp_rewards.
2
Kill-count indicator HUD Small
Show how many enemies fell this review — "Defeated 2 × Cow".
Surface result["kills"] from the burst → HUD sub-line + post-review activity message (runtime_actions.py:730).
3
Two health bars HUD Med
Player LP and current enemy LP, so the enemy's health is visible.
Add a second bar + labels in the combat branch of ui.py:1197. Enemy live LP from combat_enemy_current_lifepoints_pure over stats.hitpoints; pass through set_data's player_data.
4
Show where XP goes HUD Small
The flat XP drop hides that combat feeds Attack/Strength/Defence and Constitution. Break it out.
XP chips row driven by the burst's xp_rewards keys (attack_exp/strength_exp/defense_exp/constitution_exp), lit per active style.
5
Per-skill combat XP bars HUD Med
"No real way to see progress/xp for individual combat skills" — give them XP bars like every other skill.
Reuse compute_level_progress for attack/strength/defence/ constitution on the Combat stats screen (main menu), where non-combat skills already show theirs.
6
Menu button to the right of the bar HUD Small
It currently floats centered under the bar; move it to the bar's right edge.
New hud-right anchor in build_reviewer_js (injectors.py:54): left: calc(50% + halfHudWidth + gap) instead of the centered/edge modes. Add the radio in Settings.
7
Death indication Mechanics Small
"I apparently died and didn't even realize it." Deaths must be loud.
When result["died"] is true, fire a tooltip/dialog + activity message from handle_combat_answer, and flag the HUD state red.
8
Auto-flee option Mechanics Med
Optionally leave combat when HP is too low and you can't heal (out of food), so you stop dying silently.
New config toggle; checked in the burst / post-burst path. Triggers a leave (clear combat_encounter, drop to safe) with a notice. Threshold decision below.
9
Attack style labels its XP HUD Small
The style selector should say which XP each style trains.
Append "— trains Attack", "— trains Strength", etc. to the style combo entries in the combat setup panel (ui.py style_row ~2250).
10
Re-equip on reclaim Mechanics Med
Reclaiming gear from death should auto-re-equip the same items you had on.
In the grave/death-office reclaim path, snapshot the equipped set at death (_combat_apply_pvm_death, logic_pure.py:1303) and re-apply it on reclaim instead of dumping everything to the pack.
11
Resolve the LP-fluctuation / parity question Mechanics Decision
The bar goes up/down — "bank healing wasn't introduced till 2013." Confirmed: it's not bank healing. It's apply_safe_combat_recovery_pure (+100 LP per safe review, a full heal at Con 10) — an out-of-combat rest heal, a tuning knob.
Decision below: keep as-is, or slow toward RS-classic natural regen.

  1. Engine — chain kills + telemetry. Rework the burst loop to respawn-and-continue; return kills, aggregated drops & per-skill xp_rewards, and a clear died. Unit-test headless in .venv-qt.
  2. Engine — death & recovery. Loud death signal, auto-flee check, snapshot-and-re-equip on reclaim, and the safe-recovery relabel/tune per your call.
  3. HUD — two bars + XP chips + kill line. Rebuild the combat branch of set_data; verify with geometry probes (offscreen rule) then a Pinchtab play-test.
  4. HUD — menu button anchor + style labels + Combat XP bars. hud-right anchor, style-combo XP labels, per-skill XP bars on the stats screen.
