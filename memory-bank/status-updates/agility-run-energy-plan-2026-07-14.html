The mechanic in three moves

AnkiScape already charges a discrete "bank run" when the 28-slot pack fills or a recipe runs dry — that spent review is the walk. Agility reduces it.

01 · TODAY

The bank run is flat

One review per 28 actions, everywhere. A made-up 3.6% that's near-zero for Fletching (you stand on the bank) and enormous for Runecrafting (the walk is the whole skill). carried_bank_run_gate_pure.

02 · DERIVE

Trip cost from the source

Replace the flat constant with R/actions_per_hour − 1, so each skill's overhead is its real sourced dead time. Fractions resolve through the cadence kernel's existing rational scheduler. Also kills a latent double-count.

03 · REDUCE

Agility shrinks the walk

Level-scaled reduction of the trip cost, applied only when a bank cost is actually due. You ran the trip instead of walking it. Bags still help — the effects are sub-additive.

The one implementation invariant

Agility waives the review cost. It must never skip the bank transaction. On a successful roll the code still repacks, refills, materialises urns, deposits, and resets churn exactly as today — it simply doesn't charge a review. Sol's regression pass found five ways a naive "proc means act" version corrupts state: processing acts on an empty pack, urn charges vanish, churn re-triggers, priming becomes a free-refill exploit, and combat proceeds with no supplies. All avoided by treating this as an atomic cost-waiver, not a gate bypass.
