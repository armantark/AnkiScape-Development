DROP Promethium platebody — better than your bound Fractite 2h?

Appears only when a drop beats what's bound — rare because bind caps are authentic and drops are rolls. Ignore it and the remembered choice (or Skip) applies on your next answer.

3Never per-review — automated microplay

Eating, potion sips, door-puzzle solving, gathering, cooking, ferrying items: resolved inside the room beat under your saved policies, MTA-style. The result line tells you what happened; undo reverses all of it.
