01 The four-stage parity pipeline

Utility hub

Grind

0 XP · no level gate
Pestle & mortar crushes a source item 1:1 (horn→dust, scale→dust, nest→crushed nest, mud rune→ground). Toolless, following make_soft_clay.
GrindAction.kt · PestleAndMortarData.kt
Herblore · group 1

Clean

+ herb XP · always succeeds
Grimy herb → clean herb, gated on HerbData.levelRequirement.
clean_herb.plugin.kts:28 addXp(HERBLORE, def.experience)
Herblore · group 2

Unfinished

0 XP · herb-level gate
Clean herb + Vial of water → unf potion. Kept in the skill menu (herblore-exclusive; GE-tradeable later).
CreateUnfinishedPotionAction.kt (no addXp)
Herblore · group 3

Finished

+ recipe XP
Unf + secondary → potion, gated on PotionData.levelRequirement.
CreateFinishedPotionAction.kt:39 addXp(HERBLORE, potion.experience)
Why grind is the odd one out: it earns no XP and isn't herblore-exclusive, so it belongs in the Utility hub. Unf earns no XP but is exclusive, so — per your call — it stays in the skill menu rather than being demoted to utility.

02 Coverage — register everything, lock nothing

31 herbs

17 standard · 5 juju · 9 dungeoneering. Each yields a clean + unf action.

~90 recipes

Standard, barbarian mixes, juju, dungeoneering T1–T3. Reuse the 9 existing combat-potion product names; register the rest.

6 grinds

Unicorn horn, desert goat horn, blue dragon scale, chocolate bar, bird's nest, mud rune → dust/crushed.

0 backend locks

No explicit gates. Anything behind an unbuilt skill (Farming, Fishing, Daemonheim, juju) stays naturally input-starved — live but inert, exactly like the potions are today.

04 Craftable at launch ≈ 20 potions

Ready with today's economy

  • Attack / Super attack — eye of newt
  • Strength / Super strength — limpwurt root
  • Defence / Super defence — white berries
  • Antipoison / Super antipoison — unicorn horn dust
  • Restore / Super restore — red spiders' eggs (+ snapdragon after herb port)
  • Energy — chocolate dust
  • Prayer / Fishing — snape grass
  • Extreme attack / strength / defence — clean avantoe / dwarf weed / lantadyme

Unlocked by the ports in §03

  • Combat — goat horn dust (grind desert goat horn)
  • Antifire / Weapon poison — dragon scale dust (grind blue dragon scale)
  • Ranging — wine of zamorak (ported drop)
  • Extreme magic — ground mud runes (grind mud rune)
Still starved after launch: magic (potato cactus), all barbarian mixes (caviar/roe), all juju, all dungeoneering — each waits on a skill that doesn't exist yet. No code stops them; they simply have no inputs.
