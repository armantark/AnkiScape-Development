Not Done

Minute-detail parity remains a real backlog

This slice makes the model real and removes silent completion for every catalog quest, but the audit still calls out the work needed before saying every quest is modeled down to the smallest step.

Deferred parity gaps

Full per-stage generated extraction, quest item transformations, route and puzzle state, follower and partner logic, branch choices, complete local combat stats, and a real combat resolver are still separate work.
