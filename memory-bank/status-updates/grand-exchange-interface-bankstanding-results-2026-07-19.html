What is live

Complete offer lifecycle

Buy and owned-item sell search, previews, placement, price edits, remaining-quantity edits, cancellation, partial collection, and collect-all.

Safe by default

Collapsed configurable confirmation thresholds cover high totals and extreme price deviations without making ordinary trades noisy.

Review-driven market

Offer age advances once per answered card. No wall clock and no Bankstanding acceleration.

Non-modal collection

A persistent green HUD notice appears when any offer has collectible contents and never interrupts review.

Ironman boundary

Ironman locks only offer creation and cannot activate while an offer remains open or collectible.

Source restraint

No unauthenticated trend graph, visual market chart, eight-slot mode, or ±20% shortcuts were retained.

Atomic material-exhaustion handoff

1.
Selected action runs out of required materials.
2.
The player receives one clear warning.
3.
Future reviews switch to no-XP Bankstanding.
4.
The whole answered-card transaction saves once and undoes together.
