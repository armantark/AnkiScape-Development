The shooting star

Choosing the population fixed

"Why would I be able to choose the population of a shooting star? That's supposed to be abstracted into an S-curve over time."

It was a world-wide simulation setting shared with Big Chinchompa, surfaced on one activity's panel — which is most of why it read wrong. It is gone as a player dial. Each activity now grows its own population.

Advanced mode fixed

"I also don't understand what advanced mode is supposed to mean. Why would you not just mine the most highly available one for you always?"

It let you choose which star sizes spawn, which no player could ever do. Removed. Saved preferences from old saves are discarded rather than honoured, so a legacy setting cannot quietly keep constraining the simulation.

Nearly 3,000 XP in a burst correct as-is

"Does the shooting star really give you that much XP? It seems crazy."

It is the period first-finder bonus: 75 × your Mining level, paid in the single review that tags the star. At Mining 39 that is 2,925. Confirmed in the pinned 2012-11-15 revision and independently in Darkan's levelForXp * 75. Nothing else in the family pays more than 210, which is exactly why it looked like a bug.

Both star answers overturned your own earlier decisions. The 2026-07-10 record explicitly granted advanced mode "preferred starting sizes and a quiet/normal/busy population", and the simulation budget contract varies its seeded ceiling around that selected profile. Removing the dial meant amending that contract too. Both are recorded as supersessions rather than quiet edits — and a test now proves two otherwise-identical star events still differ, because deleting the lever without keeping its fuzziness would have made the simulation deterministic.

Skillcapes, auras and verbs

Activate cape fixed

"Does not belong there… if there is no cooldown it may as well be active as long as the cape is equipped. Can you activate it from the inventory in the real game?"

No, you cannot — and the button was inventing a mechanic outright. It granted +1 Agility for exactly ten actions. The period skillcape boost is passive while the cape is worn, has no cooldown, and does nothing from the inventory. The boost is now passive and the button is gone.

Auras need their own inventory fixed

"Auras need their own inventory in the equipment tab which you click to activate with a button."

Surefooted was already an aura — art on disk, a real cooldown field — which is why the verb felt wrong sitting beside the cape. Your cooldown rule separates them cleanly: no cooldown means passive, a cooldown means a control. Both Surefooted and Greater surefooted now live in an aura inventory that hides entirely when you own none.

Sell Pyramid top fixed

"I don't know why sell pyramid top is an action or to whom you're selling and for what reward."

1,000 coins, to Simon Templeton at the Agility Pyramid — the price was right, the buyer was simply never named. It is now a row under Named buyers with his name on it.

Leave activity fixed

"Leave activity needs to operate the same as all other activities that are meant to be left."

A real inconsistency, not a cosmetic one: Agility was the only family using a generic leave_activity command while every other used its own abandon. Now consistent.
