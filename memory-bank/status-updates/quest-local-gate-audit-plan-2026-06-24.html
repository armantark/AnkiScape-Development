Pre-Implementation Plan

Quest-local item gates audit

The goal is not to redo quest parity. This pass hunts the class of bug exposed by Vampyre Slayer: items obtained cheaply or automatically during the quest should not look like pre-owned inventory gates.

Problem Shape

False gates make playable quests feel blocked

Candidate buckets

ShopCheap, common, quest-local buys such as bar items, travel fares, and scripted NPC purchases.
NPCItems supplied by dialogue, partner contacts, route contacts, or quest set-pieces.
ParserGenerated detail text that is really a route, action, spell, skill, location, or broad prose bucket.

High-confidence means the current source-backed data already proves the item is quest-local, or a focused local source file confirms it. Ambiguous external requirements stay documented, not guessed away.
