Implementation split

Workstream checklist

1

Backend. Add a generic item-bundle claim type, seed the tutorial starter kit as unclaimed, and migrate saves through the flat claim state.

2

Frontend. Render item bundles cleanly in Bank Claims with a compact list, icon fallback from the first bundled item, and no new special panel.

3

Assets. Register and fetch missing Wooden shield and Tinderbox icons, then prove asset paths exist in tests.

4

Balance. Keep this as a one-time claim, not a recurring supply source. Later ranged and magic systems can consume the ammo/runes normally.
