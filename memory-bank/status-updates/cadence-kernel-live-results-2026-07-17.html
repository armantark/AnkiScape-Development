Design 2 survived the reconciliation

World-calendar decisions remain world-calendar decisions

Loyalty pays 100,000 points per 7,200 answered cards. Veteran capes remain 438,000 and 876,000 cards. Recurring eligibility reuses the same calendar boundary and undo contract, banking current plus one reserve entry.

Foreground duration uses actions, not fake minutes

Aura active windows deplete on eligible admitted actions; cooldowns deplete on answered-card world time. Tears of Guthix is a resumable activity-beat session. Its exact beat count and reward remain source-blocked.
