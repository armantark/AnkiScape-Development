Fish Flingers pays the real 2012 amount already

Research on the 2012 wiki history and Darkan's server source proved the payout formula is the authentic November 2012 one: level 50 paid 8,630 XP at top score in the January 2012 table, so a level-39 game pays about one level. The formula did not change and no rollback is possible or warranted. Each settled game now records its score, XP, level, and medals, capped at fifty, so any future dispute is auditable.

The perceived size comes from two clocks: ordinary skilling pays per action (trout 50 XP per card), a D&D pays its 2012 per-game sum. Changing that is a design decision on the skilling clock, not a Fish Flingers fix.
