Plan vs. reality

HELD Phase 5 economy: zero code

The plan called for adding grimy-herb combat drops and shop secondaries. In practice the economy was already at its authentic ceiling — the minorHerbTable (11 grimy herbs weighted out of 1024) was live on 22 enemy drop tables, and Darkan shops already stock the vials and reachable secondaries.

Per the strict-parity mandate, the input-starved potions (secondaries that only come from ground spawns, telegrab, Hunter, Summoning, or Farming) stay starved. No shortcuts invented. Phase 5 shipped as a no-op.

DETOUR Grind-icon regression, caught & fixed

Rewriting the grind icon loop to give the XP-less dust outputs a fallback icon accidentally dropped the input-key registrations that shop-only items (like Chocolate bar) rely on for their asset path — surfaced by the shop icon-coverage test.

Fixed by having the loop do both: register each input's own icon and give the output dust a fallback chain (input art → generic material-processing icon). All 919 tests green.
