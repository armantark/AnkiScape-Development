Frontend Starts Here

Use memory-bank/npc-shop-frontend-handoff-2026-07-06.md and shop_fixtures.build_shop_frontend_fixtures(). The backend owns prices, lock reasons, hidden rows, temporary stock, and event semantics.

Do not invent fallback prices in Qt. Visible locked rows are inspectable but inert. Hidden reward/activity shops stay out of normal browsing.
