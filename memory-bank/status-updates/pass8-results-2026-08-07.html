Direct answers Your inline Pass 8 questions

Are bones supposed to take time?

Yes. Combat auto-burial now costs two simulated game ticks for each bone or ash. Manual Prayer still uses one item for each review. The source settled the question, so no new design was necessary.

Why did Fish Flingers need two clicks?

It was a branch bug. The first click entered the event, but the selection call sat behind a different branch. One click now enters Fish Flingers and selects it for reviews.

Did the vanilla state indicator depend on “red < 10”?

No. The add-on panel covered Anki's blue, red, and green state indicator. The new layout keeps the add-on strip outside the vanilla answer area. Your playtest is the final native check for this repair.

Why is the skill guide not in Skills?

The first note named Skills. The later design session corrected the surface to Stats. The guide now fills the empty region below the selected skill's Stats block.

Do tooltips show equip and consume requirements?

Equipment requirements now show. The audited registries do not define a separate consume-level requirement. The interface does not invent one. A future sourced consume requirement can use the same player-facing boundary.

What did “hybrid Combat selector” mean?

Filtered results below 25 rows use a flat striped list. Results at 25 rows or more use the grouped tree. Both keep compact spacing and a green, bold saved target.
