Implementation

What changed

Backend

`ClaimDefinition.items`, `claim_rows_pure`, and `apply_claim_pure` now handle `item_bundle` without changing single-item or XP-lamp claims.

Frontend

Bank Claims renders bundle titles without fake `x1`, shows a compact stack preview, and exposes the full bundle in tooltips.

Assets

`combatitems/wooden_shield.png` and `combatitems/tinderbox.png` are fetched and wired through `COMBAT_ITEM_IMAGES`.

Balance

The kit is optional, one-time, no XP, no coins, and marked for migration/deletion if a real Tutorial Island loop is later added.
