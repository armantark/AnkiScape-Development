## Disputed item

The brief names the refusal seam at lines 2676-2696. The task constraint asks for "every refusal due only to a live Encounter". Two more such refusals exist outside that range, in `refill_combat_supply_loadout_pure` (line 2726) and `unload_combat_supply_loadout_pure` (line 2755). **I kept both.** Deleting them gives the player full bank access in mid-fight through any method change, which contradicts ruling 2: a bank run is the only way to the bank, and a bank run now ends the encounter. Keeping them costs nothing for ruling 1, because `reconcile_combat_method_pure` no longer calls them while an enemy is alive. If the owner wants the manual Refill and Unload buttons open in mid-fight as well, that is a two-line deletion plus three test updates.
