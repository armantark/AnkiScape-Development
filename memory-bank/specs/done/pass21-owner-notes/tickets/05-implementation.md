# Ticket 05 implementation record

The Bank owns carried rune counts. `carried_inventory[target_id]` limits the runes available during a fight; spending one reduces both that view and the Bank once. Packing adds a view, never another owned balance. Each rune type uses one of the 28 pack slots. Mandatory rune stacks displace food when needed; other carried items retain their slots. Entering and leaving Fight Caves or Barbarian Assault transfers the active pack with the combat target.

Ammunition is an item name in `equipment['ammunition']` plus `equipment_ammunition_count`. It occupies no pack slot. Casual packing chooses the highest ranged strength the weapon and levels allow, keeps a stocked worn type, and tops it up at a bank run. When that type is exhausted, restocking selects another compatible type. The existing 10× out-of-ammo danger stop remains. Clear inventory deposits the carried pack and other supplies; it leaves worn ammunition equipped. Ordinary equipment stats include ammunition, while the combat roll counts the selected projectile's strength once.

Schema 127 removes the old `ammo` and `runes` sections before combat sanitization. It returns every old rune and unselected ammunition balance to the Bank, moves the best compatible loaded ammunition to the worn slot, and preserves the single arrow already worn by the old schema. The record's unrelated food, potion, finisher, hazard and cannonball sections remain. There is no old-ammo/rune reader or writer outside the versioned migration.
