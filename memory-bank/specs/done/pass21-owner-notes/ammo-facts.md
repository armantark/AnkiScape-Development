**READ — Darkan calculation.** `calculateAmmoRecoveryOutcome` accepts a roll in `0..99`; cape **10498** retains rolls below 60, and **10499, 20068, 20769, 20771, 14641, 14642** retain rolls below 72; the next 20 rolls break the projectile, the rest drop it. Thus these are unconditional percentages, not “72% of the surviving 80%”. `dropAmmo` calls that function, removes broken/ground ammunition from equipment, and creates a ground item for the latter. It handles `quantity=2` as one recovery roll for the whole quantity; that is a **Darkan grouping behavior**, not proof that 2012 rolled a dark bow’s arrows together. ## C. Runes, pouches, rune holders and rune savings
