## Owner-settled model (2026-07-18)

- A game is a bounded brewing race: the player's duty beats (gather water,
  sweetgrass, and bark; keep stills lit; brew) produce rum units against a
  simulated opposing team brewing at a bounded, jittered rate. Highest total
  at the event-scaled budget wins.
- **Sabotage is two-way.** The opposing team occasionally disrupts the
  player's line at a bounded, jittered rate — a fire or loose monkey turns the
  next review into a repair duty instead of production — and the player may
  divert a review to sabotage the opponents, trading production for slowing
  their brew rate. Both directions resolve as ordinary duty beats.
- Pieces of eight pay per source participation/win rules into the shared
  wallet; the cosmetic catalog routes through Activity Rewards.
