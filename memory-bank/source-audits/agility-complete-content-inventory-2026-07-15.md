# Agility complete-content inventory — 2026-07-15

Status: **design-family coverage closed; historical verification and value
harvest remain open where marked.**
Cutoff: `2012-11-20T00:00:00Z`.

This inventory answers a narrower question than the existing XP and failure
audits: *what has to exist in the Agility design at all?* A missing value may be
`source_blocked`, and a positional or timed mechanic may require a labeled
AnkiScape abstraction, but neither is permission to erase a period feature.

Evidence labels:

- `period`: established under the full source-verification standard, including
  a UTC revision timestamp and resolved dependency/value evidence;
- `period_candidate`: a pinned pre-cutoff page establishes a strong design lead,
  but the row has not yet received a complete literal-wikitext/dependency ledger;
- `accepted`: already settled in an AnkiScape contract;
- `provisional`: a named modern/emulator value retained only as a replaceable
  hidden-roll candidate;
- `abstraction`: an owner-authorized review-native mapping, never a period
  claim;
- `source_blocked`: the feature exists, but a concrete value or branch remains
  unresolved;
- `dependency`: another owning system must supply the action, item, or state;
- `excluded`: explicitly outside the frozen live game or AnkiScape's product
  boundary.

## Coverage finding

`source-audits/agility-tables-2026-07-13.md` closes the obstacle/XP tables for
Gnome, Barbarian, Wilderness, Burthorpe, Pyramid, Ape Atoll, Werewolf,
Dorgesh-Kaan, Penguin, Brimhaven, and five reconstructed shortcuts. It is not a
complete Agility-content manifest. The period course navigation also contains
Bandos's Throne Room, Werewolf Skullball, Gnome Ball, and Flash Powder Factory;
The Pit is a course-triggered D&D. The complete shortcut universe is far larger
than the five emulator handlers. [Agility course oldid

template oldid
6164175](https://runescape.wiki/w/Template:Agility_courses?oldid=6164175).

The historical Rogues' Den maze is deliberately not a second live activity:
Flash Powder Factory replaced it on 2011-12-13, before the freeze. Its Rogue
reward lineage survives through the factory. [Rogues' Den oldid

## Quest and generic reward activation

The current catalog already stores many Agility requirements, direct rewards,
and choice lamps, but catalog presence remains scaffolding rather than proof of
quest fidelity. The design requirement is generic and exact:

1. making Agility trainable activates the existing skill-requirement path;
2. existing hidden `agility_exp` is preserved;
3. `pending_skill_exp_rewards["agility_exp"]` is applied once, removed once,
   and the level recalculated (`storage_pure.py:461-498`); and
4. future quest/action/claim XP goes directly through the shared reward path.

Any quest touched for a course/activity gate still needs its own pinned
verification under `pre-eoc-source-verification-standard.md`.

The aggregate reward audit also exposes three concrete catalog omissions that
the implementation thread must reconcile rather than lose: Royal Trouble
`5,000`, Grim Tales `6,000`, and optional Myths of the White Lands `10,000`
Agility XP. [Quest XP oldid
6549040](https://runescape.wiki/w/Quests/Experience_rewards?oldid=6549040).

## Remaining implementation-time source sweep

These are value questions, not coverage questions:

1. every non-Brimhaven failure curve, fail XP, damage, and exact setback still
   marked `source_blocked`;
2. Pyramid textual order and Bandos cache/object/failure details;
3. Dorgesh requested-part identity and mixed-route payout;
4. Skullball action-count/score conversion, Gnome Ball scoring/tackle rolls,
   complete FPF score/reward caps, and The Pit XP matrices/trigger chance;
5. complete Circus trick/reward table, Toy mouse row, Blast Furnace conveyor,
   and Livid Farm Agility reward pins;
6. barehand-butterfly XP/rolls and fishing double-catch probability;
7. every shortcut XP/failure conflict and grapple break chance;
8. equipment charges, stacking, exclusions, boost durations, and diary effects;
9. every visible/reward item asset from a period cache or pinned file revision;
   and
10. target-specific Prayer travel cost, plus any still-unweighted target load.

If exhaustive searching still fails, the implementation contract requires the
smallest labeled abstraction that preserves the feature's identity. It never
permits a silent omission or a claim of historical closure.
