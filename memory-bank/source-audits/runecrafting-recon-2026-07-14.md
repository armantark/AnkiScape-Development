# Runecrafting XP-rate reconnaissance — 2026-07-14

Cutoff: immediately before EOC, `2012-11-20T00:00:00Z`. This is reconnaissance, not a final rate ledger. Tier 1 below means the local 2011Scape source at `~/Downloads/game-main`; Tier 2 means literal RuneScape Wiki wikitext pinned by `oldid`.

## Verdict

**Partially sourceable.** Pre-EOC Tier-2 rates exist for ordinary elemental routes, several Nature routes, combination runes, Ourania/ZMI, Blood/Astral crafting, and Runespan. The cleanest standard-altar candidate is the internally consistent Nature fairy-ring-out / glory-back route: **11,600 XP/hour at Runecrafting 50 with 43 essence per trip**, small+medium+large pouches, and no essence familiar ([Tier 2, oldid 6530445](https://runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Runecrafting_training?oldid=6530445)). Runespan also has explicit condition-bound rates and was released on **2012-04-30**, before the cutoff ([Tier 2, oldid 6563925](https://runescape.wiki/w/Runespan?oldid=6563925)).

The central requested comparison does **not** close: the pre-EOC Nature guide describes an Abyss route but gives it no XP/hour, and the Abyss article describes the route but gives no trip time or Nature rate. Therefore the on-foot-versus-Abyss empirical delta is `source_blocked`, not inferred. Law and Death standard-altar XP/hour rates are likewise absent from the scoped primary pages. Several other quoted rates are usable leads but not yet house-standard because their pouch, familiar, outfit, repair-time, or route assumptions are not fully stated.

Tier 1 establishes the mechanics around the rates, not the rates themselves. `RunecraftAction.kt` consumes the entire selected essence stack in one craft; `pouches.plugin.kts` supplies capacities and degradation behavior. No Tier-1 XP/hour, circuit-time, or essence-per-circuit calculation was found.

## Essence per action

Tier 1 and Tier 2 agree on the four standard pouch capacities and unlock levels: small `3` at RC 1, medium `6` at RC 25, large `9` at RC 50, and giant `12` at RC 75 (`pouches.plugin.kts`; [Tier 2, oldid 6312549](https://runescape.wiki/w/Runecrafting_pouches?oldid=6312549)). Because every pouch occupies one of the 28 inventory slots, the net full-runner action sizes are:

| RC level | Pouches carried | Inventory essence | Pouched essence | **N: essence/action** |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | small | 27 | 3 | **30** |
| 25 | small+medium | 26 | 9 | **35** |
| 50 | small+medium+large | 25 | 18 | **43** |
| 75 | small+medium+large+giant | 24 | 30 | **54** |

An abyssal parasite/lurker/titan can add 7 essence, taking the level-75 standard load to **61**, but that familiar is a separate Summoning dependency and must not be baked into a base Runecrafting action ([Tier 2, oldid 6312549](https://runescape.wiki/w/Runecrafting_pouches?oldid=6312549); [Abyss oldid 6313726](https://runescape.wiki/w/Abyss?oldid=6313726)). A carried talisman or teleport-rune stack can reduce the inventory portion; an equipped tiara/hood or Abyss rift avoids that slot cost.

Consequently the empirical run-energy magnitude is **unknown**, not `11.6k ÷ guessed Abyss rate`. The available 5,800 versus 11,600 Nature figures compare two non-Abyss fairy-ring return patterns and therefore cannot answer the requested Abyss effect ([Tier 2, oldid 6530445](https://runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Runecrafting_training?oldid=6530445)).

## Pouch degradation

The period fact is secure: the small pouch never decays; larger pouches lose capacity on filling, can continue degrading, and can be repaired by the Dark mage or remotely through NPC Contact ([Tier 2, oldids 6312549](https://runescape.wiki/w/Runecrafting_pouches?oldid=6312549) and [6313726](https://runescape.wiki/w/Abyss?oldid=6313726)). The exact counters conflict:

- **Tier-1 lead only, not a finding:** `pouches.plugin.kts` begins capacity loss after degradation counters 10/34/44 for giant/large/medium. Those code values have no matching oldid and conflict with the period pages below.
- Tier 2 `Runecrafting pouches` gives 10/29/45 full uses ([oldid 6312549](https://runescape.wiki/w/Runecrafting_pouches?oldid=6312549)).
- Tier 2 `Abyss` says 11 full giant-pouch trips and degradation on the 12th, after 135 essence ([oldid 6313726](https://runescape.wiki/w/Abyss?oldid=6313726)).

This recon does not reconcile those counters. For rate provenance, most quotes merely list pouches and do not demonstrate that repair time is included. The Nature profit table explicitly excludes **repair costs**, not necessarily repair time; treat `pouch_decay_repair` as **not proven included** until the full harvest closes each method.
