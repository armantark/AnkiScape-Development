# Gap Grill 13: Fishing Activities Provenance

Cutoff: RuneScape immediately before EOC, `2012-11-20`.

## Pinned period evidence

- `Fishing Trawler` oldid 6288357, 2012-09-24: level 15; safe team minigame;
  twelve-minute trip; swamp paste, rope, and bailing-bucket duties; torn nets,
  leaks, water/sinking, activity bar, successful level-shaped catch, and XP at
  40% of normal catch XP. The period page identifies level 79 sea turtles and
  level 81 manta rays.
- `Fish Flingers` oldid 6529096, 2012-11-03: no level/quest/item requirement;
  temporary bait/hook/weight controls; six species and four locations; rating
  feedback; communal depletion; cooperative big fish; Fishing-level-scaled XP;
  two daily tickets capped at ten held; tokens, medals, and reward shop.
- `Dungeoneering/Fishing` oldid 5812989, 2012-06-23: Daemonheim pond generation;
  fly rod and feathers; tiered fish; some half-XP spots; 14-fish depletion; and
  temporary fish used by the dungeon Cooking/survival loop.

All claims were read from literal archived wikitext with
`tools/wiki_parity.py`. Modern RS3 and OSRS behavior was not used as parity
evidence.

## Product boundary

Fishing Trawler is a source-shaped persistent pressure activity with a fixed
automatic public-crew baseline rather than selectable or persisted crew state;
see
`memory-bank/source-audits/fishing-trawler-computation-reconciliation-2026-07-17.md`.
Fish Flingers is a separate future multiplayer D&D design. Daemonheim fishing
cannot be implemented honestly outside the future Dungeoneering session and
temporary inventory. The global answered-card clock drives background/reset
timing but does not convert a twelve-minute Trawler trip into two skilling
reviews.
