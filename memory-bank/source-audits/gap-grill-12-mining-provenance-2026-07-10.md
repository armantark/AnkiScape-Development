# Gap Grill 12: Living Rock Caverns and Shooting Stars

Cutoff: RuneScape immediately before EOC, `2012-11-20`.

## Pinned period evidence

- `Living Rock Caverns` oldid 6547876, 2012-11-11: no entry requirement;
  dangerous aggressive creatures; busy public worlds and some safe spots;
  level-73 remains, level-77 concentrated coal, level-80 concentrated gold;
  deposit lift; public-corpse mining after a delay; and 25 XP per remains.
- `Living rock remains` oldid 6556376, 2012-11-15: level 73, 25 XP, 5–24
  Living minerals, and a one-minute wait before mining another player's corpse.
  This conflicts with the cavern overview's 5–20 range and must be reconciled
  before implementing the reward roll.
- `Shooting Star` oldid 6556329, 2012-11-15: sizes 1–9; Mining level equal to
  ten times size; fixed communal stardust per layer; layer-specific XP per dust;
  200 owned-dust cap; 75-times-level first-finder XP; once-per-UTC-day sprite
  exchange; proportional rewards up to 152 Cosmic runes, 52 Astral runes,
  20 noted Gold ore, 50,002 Coins, and a temporary 25% extra-ore effect.

All claims were read from literal archived wikitext with
`tools/wiki_parity.py`. Modern RS3 and OSRS behavior was not used as parity
evidence.

## Evidence and product boundaries

The Living-mineral quantity conflict and exact corpse/deposit timing need
reconciled period or high-fidelity server evidence. The Shooting Star layer
tables are literal. For the product abstraction, AnkiScape joins an active
communal star, treats the first-finder tag as an occasional session-start event,
and permits one manual sprite exchange per completed star. It does not add a
fabricated scouting minigame or enforce the historical UTC daily reset.
