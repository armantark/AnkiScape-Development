# Social surfaces and classic random events

Date: `2026-07-19`

Cutoff: RuneScape immediately before EOC, `2012-11-20`.

Status: **owner-settled dispositions; values are harvest work.**

## Classic random events — excluded at cutoff (final, 2026-07-19)

The classic anti-bot random events (Evil Bob, Sandwich lady, Genie, Mysterious
Old Man, Drill Demon, and the rest) were removed from the live game on
2012-10-24, before the cutoff. The owner briefly chose a nostalgia inclusion
on 2026-07-19, then reversed it the same day on cutoff-parity grounds
("they should be not present indeed but that sucks"): **excluded**, recorded
on the player-visible exclusion/nostalgia ledger with the removal date. The
removal-date pin against a period source remains harvest work. No generic
random-injection layer exists either, per packet decision 10-B.

## Bonus XP Weekends — included on the world clock (owner-settled 2026-07-19)

Bonus XP Weekends are period-authentic live-ops (first March 2010, recurring
through 2012) and are **included**: a recurring event on the answered-card
calendar at a source-shaped cadence, applying the authentic diminishing
multiplier (starting ~×2.7 and decaying with time trained during the event
toward its floor). The mechanic satisfies the reward-XP accounting rule —
sourced multiplier, bounded duration, with/without parity assertion. Exact
historical cadence, curve constants, and floor are harvest work from
pre-cutoff revisions.

## Rat Pits — reversed to a minimal cat-training surface (2026-07-19)

The earlier same-week exclusion is reversed on new consideration: wily (and
lazy) cat progression has no route without the Rat Pits, and the core loop —
your cat catching rats in the pit — is solo-shaped. The Pits return as a
minimal pet-training activity: duty beats advancing the authentic cat
training meter toward wily cat, with the lazy-cat consequence and any
period wager layer against other players' cats excluded under the social
exclusion. Training rules, meters, and outcomes are harvest work.

## Social-only surfaces — exclusions confirmed (2026-07-19)

- **Party Room:** excluded with a player-visible note — purely player-funded
  redistribution has no solo translation without fabricating a faucet.
  Revisitable later only as a labeled-invention coin-sink lottery (player
  funds the chest, net sink).
- **Burthorpe Games Room:** excluded — no material rewards in the source.
- **Rat Pits:** excluded as an activity (wagers against other players'
  cats). The wily-cat training progression it enabled is noted as a pet-system
  harvest item so cat progression does not silently dead-end; Rat Catchers
  quest stages continue through the collapsed quest pipeline.
- **Item lending and the Assist System:** remain under the social-trading
  exclusion — both are inter-player transfers with no balance-safe solo
  analogue (the Assist exclusion was already applied to Ancient Effigies).
