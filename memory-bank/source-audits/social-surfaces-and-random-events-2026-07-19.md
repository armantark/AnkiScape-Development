## Classic random events — deliberate nostalgia inclusion (owner override)

The classic anti-bot random events (Evil Bob, Sandwich lady, Genie, Mysterious
Old Man, Drill Demon, and the rest of the post-2009 roster) were removed from
the live game on 2012-10-24, before the cutoff — so strict cutoff parity
excludes them. The owner explicitly overrode that on 2026-07-19: **include
them as a labeled deliberate divergence** (the identity rule: divergences we
choose get written down).

Shape (pattern-determined, no new mechanism):

- Events surface rarely on the answered-card world clock through the settled
  contextual action row (Aksel family): one row, accept/ignore, ignoring lets
  the event leave with no penalty — matching the post-2009 source behavior
  where declining a random was safe.
- Accepting resolves the event as one review beat (or one short beat sequence
  where the source event was multi-stage, e.g. Evil Bob's island) with the
  event's sourced rewards.
- Rewards, rosters, and frequencies harvest from pre-removal wiki revisions
  (pre-2012-10-24 oldids); the roster is the post-2009 safe-decline era, not
  the punitive pre-2009 one.
- No generic random-injection layer returns: these are the authentic named
  events only, per the settled packet decision 10-B's surviving half.

## Social-only surfaces — exclusions confirmed (2026-07-19)

- **Party Room:** excluded with a player-visible note — purely player-funded
  redistribution has no solo translation without fabricating a faucet.
  Revisitable later only as a labeled-invention coin-sink lottery (player
  funds the chest, net sink).
- **Burthorpe Games Room:** excluded — no material rewards in the source.
- **Rat Pits:** excluded as an activity (wagers against other players'
  cats). The wily-cat training progression it enabled is noted as a pet-system
  harvest item so cat progression does not silently dead-end; Rat Catchers
  quest stages continue through the collapsed quest pipeline.
- **Item lending and the Assist System:** remain under the social-trading
  exclusion — both are inter-player transfers with no balance-safe solo
  analogue (the Assist exclusion was already applied to Ancient Effigies).
