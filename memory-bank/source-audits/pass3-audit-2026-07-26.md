**Result: 37 clauses driven, 16 fail.** The split is clean and it is the whole

So the spec exists, it is unambiguous, and nothing was lost. It was queued and
never scheduled. Bork is separately blocked on the same missing substrate

### Method

Conformance was driven, not read. Player state was built through real runtime
paths — `handle_mining_answer`, `handle_smithing_answer`, `handle_magic_answer`,
`handle_woodcutting_answer`, `handle_fishing_answer` with a real
`RuntimeActionContext` — and the resulting ledger, predicates and completion
names were read back. The undo clause loaded the add-on package with the
integration-smoke fakes and used the real
`_capture_player_data_snapshot` / `_remember_review_award_snapshot` /
`_restore_review_award_snapshot` seam.

## 2. Shooting Star experience

**Verdict: correct as-is. Not a bug, and not a cadence abstraction.** The ~3,000
XP burst is the period first-finder bonus, reproduced exactly.

### What the period paid

Pinned: `Shooting Star`, oldid `6556329`, 2012-11-15T09:20:35Z
(<https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/6556329>). Verified directly
against the pinned revision, not recalled.

The revision states, verbatim: "The first person who attempts to mine the star
(even if they do not have the required mining level) will be rewarded with
Mining experience in the amount of 75 times your mining level."

Per-stardust experience and layer capacity, from the same revision:

| Size | Mining level | XP per stardust | Stardust in layer |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 9 | 90 | 210 | 15 |
| 8 | 80 | 145 | 25 |
| 7 | 70 | 114 | 40 |
| 6 | 60 | 71 | 80 |
| 5 | 50 | 47 | 175 |
| 4 | 40 | 32 | 250 |
| 3 | 30 | 29 | 439 |
| 2 | 20 | 25 | 700 |
| 1 | 10 | 14 | 1,200 |

A size-9 star mined to exhaustion by one player is 2,924 stardust and 80,271
Mining XP.

Corroborated independently by Darkan at commit
`c95aca409f397cab12c13b6d5269b73f6cd8abe9`: the same nine XP values in
`src/main/java/com/rs/game/content/skills/mining/Ore.java:121-145`, and
`levelForXp * 75` for the first finder in
`src/main/java/com/rs/game/content/dnds/shootingstar/ShootingStars.java:140-151`.
Darkan's layer lives come from a formula
(`Star.java:32-48`) and are a communal depletion timer rather than a stardust
count, so they do not corroborate the capacity column; the wiki table stands
alone there.

game-main has no Shooting Star implementation — only cache-derived identities.
The 727, 741 and 742 caches supply Stardust (13727), Crashed star (38660-38668)
and Star sprite (8091) identities and nothing numeric, as expected.

### What we pay

`data/shooting_star_data.py:39-60` reproduces the period layer table exactly,
row for row, and cites the same oldid at lines 9-16. Per-review payout is
`layer.xp_per_dust + tag_xp` (`logic/shooting_star_logic.py:590-611`), with
`tag_xp = 75 * mining_level` awarded once on the first attempt when the player
wins the tag (`logic/shooting_star_logic.py:521-543`).

Per-star totals match the period table at every starting size, 16,800 through
80,271, with zero difference on all nine rows.

### Where ~3,000 in one burst comes from

The tag. `75 × 39 = 2,925`; `75 × 40 = 3,000`. A player around Mining 40 who
wins the first-finder tag receives that in the single review that tags the star,
on top of the layer's ordinary 14-210. Nothing else in the family can produce a
burst near that size — the largest ordinary review payout is 210.

That figure is period-attested by two independent sources and reproduced
exactly. It is startling because the period bonus was startling.

### Not established

The Star Sprite's own experience reward. `Star sprite`, oldid `6225869`,
2012-09-09T22:14:40Z, says only that sprites exchange stardust "in exchange for
Mining experience and rewards", with no amount, formula, level scaling or cap.
The detailed Shooting Star revision does not list sprite XP at all. Darkan's
exchange path pays coins, runes, gold ore and the 15-minute ore effect with no
`addXp` call. game-main has nothing.

AnkiScape pays **0** Mining XP for the sprite release and exchange
(`logic/shooting_star_logic.py:257-291`, `615-661`), which is consistent with
every source we can read but is not positively proven. Searched: Darkan, all
three near-cutoff caches, game-main, and four pinned wiki revisions across
2008-2012. This is a gap in the record, not a value to estimate — and because
nothing on disk states a number, it is not a placeholder either.

### Our gate

There is exactly one Smoking Kills condition in the Python sources:
`ui/ui.py:8519`, controlling the Rewards subview. It is narrower than the
period's, not wider — it gates presentation only, because the point economy it
would gate does not exist yet. `logic/slayer_logic.py:157-159` sanitizes
`slayer_points` and `slayer_qualifying_streak` but nothing ever awards or spends
them, and `slayer_logic.py:272-273` already documents that Turael tasks award no
points and do not advance the post-quest streak.

The coin-based equipment shops are correctly ungated: all eight
`*_slayer_equipment` shops in `data/shop_data.py` use currency `Coins`, carry an
empty `lock_reason`, and gate only on their own travel quests where the period

Peril). None stocks the Ring of slaying, which was point-priced.

This all matches `memory-bank/slayer-skill-contract-2026-07-15.md:437-446`,
which already settled the same boundary.

No code change, and therefore no new test.

## Addendum — the diary gap was closed on 2026-07-26

Section 1 above reported sixteen unbuilt diary clauses. Eleven of them now hold,
built on a source the audit did not look for: **Darkan carries the whole period
achievement system** in `com/rs/game/content/achievements`. `Achievement.java`
is the task id/name enum, one `<Region>Tasks` class per family holds the exact
per-tier task-id lists, and `SetReward.java` holds the eight reward item id sets.
Several tier reward effects are there too — `dailyBattlestaffLimit`,
`coalTruckCapacity`, `bertSandAmount`, `enhancedExcaliburDuration`.

The audit's own evidence order puts Darkan first, so the wiki was never the right
starting point for this family. The pinned pre-cutoff wiki revisions still own the
per-task requirement columns, which Darkan reads from the cache at runtime.

On five of the eight families the two sources agree on every tier's task count
exactly, which is what made the join safe to automate.

See `memory-bank/achievement-diaries-2026-07-26.md` for the clause-by-clause
result and for the five clauses that remain NOT MET.
