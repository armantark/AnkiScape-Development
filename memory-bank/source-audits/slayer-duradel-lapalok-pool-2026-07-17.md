## Master succession and challenge

Duradel/Lapalok's point schedule produces a steady-state 50-task average of
24 points:

`(45 × 15 + 4 × 75 + 225) / 50 = 24`.

The first four tasks after beginning or resetting a streak award zero, so this
is a repeating-cycle average rather than a fresh-account first-50 average.
Lapalok succession changes NPC presentation only: it must preserve the
assignment, streak, points and challenge ledger.

The optional challenge is a single Slayer Tower trip containing **69 kills**:

- 21 Crawling hands;
- 8 Banshees;
- 9 Infernal mages;
- 7 Bloodvelds;
- 7 Aberrant spectres;
- 5 Gargoyles;
- 6 Nechryael; and
- 6 Abyssal demons.

It requires Slayer 85, the required Banshee/Spectre protection and a rock
hammer. `Slayer/Challenges` oldid `6328439`, timestamp
`2012-10-01T08:55:32Z`, explicitly says the individual kills award no Slayer
XP; hand-in awards **20,000 Slayer XP**, **35 bonus points**, and one task:
<https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer/Challenges?oldid=6328439>.
The resulting point totals are **50 / 110 / 260** on ordinary, tenth and
fiftieth milestones.

The master pages' `24,556–24,640 XP` sentence contradicts the dedicated
challenge page and is rejected as stale arithmetic. The exact offer
prerequisite, probability and any literal tower-exit grace timer remain
`source_blocked`. The owner-authorized five-task/10% offer fallback remains
labeled implementation-only.
