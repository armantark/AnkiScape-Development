# Cooking 2011Scape Source Audit - 2026-06-16

## Scope Decision

Cooking v1 implements every XP-bearing row from the local 2011Scape rev 667
`CookingData.kt` table. Each AnkiScape review action consumes exactly one raw or
prepared input. On success it produces the cooked item and awards source Cooking
XP; on failure it consumes the input, produces the source burnt item, and awards
no XP.

Fire/range objects are intentionally not exposed as a player choice. The source
stores only one low/high chance pair per food and `CookingAction.kt` uses that
pair for both fire and range interactions, so AnkiScape keeps one Cooking target
per source output path.

## Implemented Source Rows

`cooking_data.py` captures 54 stable targets from `CookingData.kt`, preserving
raw item, cooked item, burnt item, level, base XP, low chance, and high chance.

Implemented families:

- Fish: shrimps through rocktail, including cavefish, sea turtle, and manta ray.
- Eels and snails: cave eel, slimy eel, thin/lean/fat snail.
- Bread: bread and pitta bread.
- Pies: redberry, meat, mud, apple, garden, fish, admiral, wild, and summer.
- Stews/curry: stew and curry.
- Vegetables/pizza: baked potato, scrambled egg, cooked sweetcorn, fried onions,
  fried mushrooms, and plain pizza.
- Meat: rabbit, chicken, beef, bear, rat, ugthanki, yak.

The source's `secondaryCooked` behavior for beef and bear is exposed as two
distinct targets:

- `cook_beef` and `cook_bear_meat` produce `Cooked meat`.
- `render_beef_sinew` and `render_bear_sinew` produce `Sinew`.

This keeps output-colliding actions selectable by stable IDs instead of display
names.

## Source Mechanics Translation

`CookingAction.kt` removes the raw item before rolling success. It then checks
whether interpolated `lowChance..highChance` beats `RANDOM.nextInt(255)`.
AnkiScape preserves the source low/high values in `COOKING_DATA`, then adapts
them through a review-scale success curve in `logic_pure.py`. This mirrors the
Mining/Woodcutting/Fishing approach: source odds remain audited facts, and
review pacing stays an AnkiScape balance layer.

Burnt outputs are real inventory items, but they are economy junk in this fork:
they are registered as `food` and `tradeable=False` so a future GE/trading
system does not accidentally treat them as marketable items.

## Utility Bridge

No-XP Cooking prep lives under Utility/Activities, not Cooking XP:

- Gathering bridges: grain, potatoes, onions, eggs, redberries, cooking apples,
  tomatoes, cheese, cabbage, mushrooms, sweetcorn, spice, raw meats, and snails.
- Milling/dough: `Grain + Empty pot -> Pot of flour`, then flour to bread dough,
  pitta dough, pastry dough, or pizza base.
- Containers/water: bowl of water and bucket of water.
- Assembly: pie shell, uncooked egg, chopped onion, sliced mushrooms, pie
  fillings, uncooked stew/curry, and uncooked pizza.

Known Farming-heavy or broader-system materials such as compost, strawberry,
watermelon, and raw chompy remain ordinary material locks unless a source route
already makes sense in Utility/Activities. The recipe rows are still live; they
are simply gated by missing materials.

## Assets

`tools/fetch_cooking_assets.py` derives its manifest from `cooking_data.py` and
the registered item maps. It reuses Fishing/Crafting/Mining/Smithing art where
available, then writes Cooking-specific sprites to `cookingitems/` plus
`icon/cooking_icon.png`. The 2026-06-16 fetch resolved 132 Cooking icons with
provenance and no failures.

The shared `Burnt fish` item uses the concrete `Burnt fish (trout)` wiki file as
its representative sprite because the generic wiki title is a disambiguation
page.

## Deferred And Audit-Only Content

No XP-bearing `CookingData.kt` row is hidden or deferred. Missing routes for
some inputs are future economy/acquisition work, not special UI states.

Farming, Combat, shops, GE/trading, quests, minigames, and multiplayer behavior
are outside this slice. They can later provide cleaner acquisition routes for
the live material-locked rows without changing those Cooking targets.
