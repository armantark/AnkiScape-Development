## Pinned period evidence

`Bork`, oldid `6565904` (`2012-11-17T10:41:40Z`), establishes:

- `The Hunt for Surok` miniquest unlocks the encounter;
- Bork may be defeated once per source day;
- the first kill grants 5,000 Slayer XP;
- repeat kills grant 250 Slayer XP, or 500 after the Elite Varrock tasks;
- Elite Varrock completion also improves the coin, gem, and charm payout;
- Ring of Wealth changes the gem/charm outcome;
- defeating Bork is required for `While Guthix Sleeps`; and
- Bork fights with summoned Ork legions.
