# Gap Grill 14: Firemaking Pyres and Beacons Provenance

Cutoff: RuneScape immediately before EOC, `2012-11-20`.

## Pinned period evidence

- `Firemaking` oldid 6513361, 2012-10-31, plus `Template:Pyre ships` oldid
  5501212, 2012-04-16: Barbarian pyre ships; chewed/mangled bones; log-tier
  Firemaking/Crafting levels and XP; one-to-five enhanced Prayer actions;
  dangerous spirit when using mangled bones.
- `Shades of Mort'ton (minigame)` oldid 6449451, 2012-10-21: quest gate;
  temple repair and sanctity; olive oil to sacred oil; two-to-four oil doses per
  pyre log; period level/XP table; shade compatibility; cremation, Prayer XP,
  keys/coins, catacomb doors and chests.
- `All Fired Up` oldid 6462528, 2012-10-24: level-43 quest, 45-log quest route,
  5,500 Firemaking XP, and Beacon Network access.
- `All Fired Up (minigame)` oldid 6564841, 2012-11-17: 14 beacons; 20 identical
  unnoted logs each; optional five-log tending/refuelling; individual skill,
  quest, repair, and keeper requirements; estimated burn times from roughly 13
  to 18 minutes; simultaneous-beacon XP; and 6/10/14 reward milestones.

All claims were read from literal archived wikitext with
`tools/wiki_parity.py`. Modern RS3 and OSRS behavior was not used as parity
evidence.

## Product and evidence boundaries

Pyre-log processing and cremation can be ordinary source-backed actions even
before the larger Shades activity supplies their inputs. The temple/sanctity/
catacomb loop remains one later multi-skill activity. The Beacon Network uses a
14-row state panel and activity-phase burn windows rather than a world map or a
six-minutes-per-review shortcut. Exact shade key/coin rolls, spirit combat flow,
and beacon phase calibration remain source-blocked pending reconciled server
evidence.
