Cutoff: 2012-11-20. Owner approval, word for word: "8. Yes. Again, we need to do this for similar quest steps for things that can/should be done during the quest.", "20. Each candidate was checked against the literal saved guide and the composed quest data. Thirteen rows fold; four rows are dropped (including the duplicate Hazeel Cult candidate). The original route table remains intact. Every supply card makes one recipe batch, preserves surplus stock, skips a held product and stops before spending anything if a required coin, level, tool or input is absent. No Utilities action, item definition, art or screen layout is added. Existing products retain their registered assets.

Zero XP means the quest guide states no action XP and the supply step awards none. For Muspah, the special cave mining-and-cutting action has no numeric XP in the period guide; this slice does not substitute ordinary gem mining or cutting XP, nor invent a Crafting gate. The existing quest completion award is separate. Muspah’s twelve-gem ending correction remains outside this ticket.
