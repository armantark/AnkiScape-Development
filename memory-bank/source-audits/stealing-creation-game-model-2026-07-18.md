## Owner-settled model (2026-07-18)

The session keeps all three source legs inside the packet's settled frame
(temporary closed economy, bounded opponent simulation, session substrate):

- **Gather/process** duty beats collect class 1–5 sacred clay and work kilns
  into session-only tools, weapons, armour, and barriers; deposited/processed
  clay builds the player's score.
- **Fight**: simulated opposing raiders drawn from a period-shaped population
  (reusing the Fist of Guthix bounded-opponent machinery) engage the player as
  live six-tick encounters at a bounded, jittered rate. Crafted session
  weapons and armour are actually equipped in these fights — the consumer that
  makes crafting choices meaningful — and death loses carried clay to the
  opposing score under the source rule.
- **Race**: the deposited-clay total runs against the opposing team's bounded
  rate to decide the win, following the Trouble Brewing race pattern.
- Points buy the volatile/morphic double-XP tools; those are XP-multiplying
  rewards and stay hidden until the cadence invariant accounts for them
  (packet decision 12-B).
