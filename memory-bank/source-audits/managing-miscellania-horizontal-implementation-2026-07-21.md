## Live mechanic boundary

Managing Miscellania is an Activities-only persistent management surface gated
by `Throne of Miscellania`. `Royal Trouble` upgrades the subject count, coffer
cap, maximum salary, and approval decay exactly as pinned by the contract.

The implementation includes:

- a `240`-answered-card kingdom day with no wall-clock input;
- approval decay before the day's salary and production calculation;
- explicit exact deposit/withdraw, Fund 1/7/30 days, and Deposit maximum;
- ten or fifteen subjects, a shared pool, and the source ten-worker family cap;
- retroactive conversion of the complete unclaimed credit pool through the
  allocation and output choice visible at collection;
- one eligible saved approval-work chooser and a one-review Maple chore;
- direct-banked atomic collection;
- schema-67 persistence with no migration windfall;
- whole-review undo for day ticks and approval work, plus direct-action undo
  for every coffer, allocation, output-choice, and collection command;
- cadence, telemetry, quest-gate, and target-reachability registration; and
- one compact Landmarks detail card inside the existing four-page Activities
  pager.
