## 1. Wyson the gardener

Source: Wyson the gardener, revision **6226377**, **2012-09-09T22:53:00Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Wyson_the_gardener?oldid=6226377](https://runescape.wiki/w/Wyson_the_gardener?oldid=6226377).

- Location, free-player availability, and sale: `|members= No`, `|location= [[Falador Park]]`; "'''Wyson the gardener''' is the gardener in [[Falador Park]]. You can buy [[woad leaves]] from him, which are used to make [[blue dye]]."
- **15 coins buys one leaf; 20 coins buys two leaves.** Full price passage: "You may offer Wyson 5, 10, 15 or 20 [[Coins]] to buy [[Woad Leaves]]. If you offer Wyson 20 [[coins]], he will give you two [[woad leaves]] in return for being generous. If you offer 15 [[coins]], he will give you one. But if you offer him 10 or fewer coins, he won't accept the offer."
- His other documented transaction is an exchange, not another coin-priced sale: "Wyson will exchange the [[Mole skin|skins]] and [[mole claw|claws]] for [[bird nest]]s; the nests may or may not have seeds. Wyson will accept only un-noted mole skins and mole claws."
- Purchase interaction: the quoted price passage describes offering a price. The infobox also says `|shop= Yes`. This alone does **not** establish whether a normal shop window exists; dialogue and limits require further evidence.

## 2. Aggie's dye service

Source: Aggie, revision **6338366**, **2012-10-03T02:02:33Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Aggie?oldid=6338366](https://runescape.wiki/w/Aggie?oldid=6338366).

Location and membership: `|members = No`, `|location = [[Draynor Village]]`; "'''Aggie''' the witch is a [[non-player character]] in western [[Draynor Village]]".

Fee: "Aggie provides primary colours of dye for 5 coins plus ingredients." Her recipe list says:

- Red: "[[Red dye]] requires 3 [[redberries]]."
- Yellow: "[[Yellow dye]] requires 2 [[onion]]s."
- Blue: "[[Blue dye]] requires 2 [[Woad leaf|woad leaves]]."

Thus **each dye costs its ingredients plus 5 coins**, not 5 coins per ingredient. Corroboration: "Note: All dyes are purchased for 5 coins each. For blue dye, however, players typically buy 2 [[Woad leaf|Woad leaves]] from [[Wyson the gardener]] for an additional 20 coins. Members may grow their own Woad."

An earlier period quest guide explicitly documents both interactions: Goblin Diplomacy, revision **4700293**, **2011-09-04T11:37:39Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Goblin_Diplomacy?oldid=4700293](https://runescape.wiki/w/Goblin_Diplomacy?oldid=4700293).

## 3. Goblin Diplomacy

Primary source: Goblin Diplomacy, revision **6485448**, **2012-10-27T18:21:47Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Goblin_Diplomacy?oldid=6485448](https://runescape.wiki/w/Goblin_Diplomacy?oldid=6485448) (GD). ### Requirements and items

- **Free-to-play:** GD says `|members = No`.
- **No prerequisite quests or skill requirements; no required enemies.** This is the historical template's explicit default, rather than a modern guide substituted for period evidence. GD invokes `{{Quest details` with `start`, `difficulty`, `length`, `members`, `items` and `wdifficulty`, and supplies neither `requirements` nor `kills`. Template:Quest details, revision **6474282**, **2012-10-26T01:31:22Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Template:Quest_details?oldid=6474282](https://runescape.wiki/w/Template:Quest_details?oldid=6474282), defines the displayed requirements as `{{#if:{{{requirements|}}}|{{{requirements}}}|None}}` and enemies as `{{#if:{{{kills|}}}|{{{kills}}}|None}}`. Read with the GD invocation, both display **None**. This is a transclusion-based reading of the two fetched period revisions.
- **Three suits of ordinary goblin mail, one blue dye, one orange dye.** GD items passage:

> `*3 suits of [[Goblin mail]] (can be obtained during quest)`
>
> `*[[Blue dye]] (or 2 [[Woad leaf|Woad leaves]] and 5 [[coins]] to make)`
>
> `*[[Orange dye]] (or 2 [[Onion]]s, 3 [[Redberries]], and 10 coins to make)`

Starting from raw ingredients therefore requires **3 redberries, 2 onions, 2 woad leaves, and 15 coins paid to Aggie**, plus the three mails. The 15 is the sum of the three sourced 5-coin service fees. If the leaves must also be bought from Wyson, add **20 coins for the pair**, for **35 coins total** across Wyson and Aggie. These are arithmetic totals, not extra quest fees. Already owning the finished dyes removes those ingredient/coin needs.

### Steps, in order

1. **Talk to General Bentnoze or General Wartface at Goblin Village.** QG: "Talk to [[General Bentnoze]] or [[General Wartface]], both of whom are located in the [[Goblin Village]]."
2. **Obtain three goblin mails.** GD provides crate locations: "Behind the generals' hut", "Next to the western hut", "Up a ladder near the entrance". QG also says "How they are obtained does not matter."
3. **Get orange and blue dye.** QG: "Mix [[Red dye]] and [[Yellow dye]] to obtain [[Orange dye]]." Then: "If not obtained yet, get some [[Blue dye]]. How it is obtained does not matter."
4. **Make one orange mail and one blue mail; retain one ordinary brown mail.** QG: "Dye one goblin mail blue and dye another goblin mail orange." GD: "Dye one goblin mail orange, one blue, and leave the last one unaltered."
5. **Give orange mail first.** QG: "Give the [[Orange goblin mail]] to one of the generals." GD: "They will have [[Grubfoot]] try it on, and say they do not like it."
6. **Give blue mail next.** QG: "After the cutscene, give the [[Blue goblin mail]] to one of them." GD: "Again they will have Grubfoot try it on, and again it is rejected."
7. **Give unchanged brown mail last; complete the quest.** QG: "After another cutscene, give the normal goblin mail to one of them." Then: "Quest complete!" GD: "They decide that the original colour, brown, is best after all."

**How to dye the mail:** Orange goblin mail, revision **6435504**, **2012-10-17T19:20:23Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Orange_goblin_mail?oldid=6435504](https://runescape.wiki/w/Orange_goblin_mail?oldid=6435504): "'''Orange goblin mail''' is made by using [[orange dye]] on [[goblin mail]]." Blue goblin mail, revision **6435506**, **2012-10-17T19:21:33Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Blue_goblin_mail?oldid=6435506](https://runescape.wiki/w/Blue_goblin_mail?oldid=6435506): "'''Blue goblin mail''' is made by using [[blue dye]] on [[goblin mail]]." Brown is the unchanged mail, not another dye recipe.

### Differences between the fetched period revisions

- The older GD revision **4700293**, **2011-09-04T11:37:39Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Goblin_Diplomacy?oldid=4700293](https://runescape.wiki/w/Goblin_Diplomacy?oldid=4700293), lists "[[Blue dye]] '''and''' [[Orange dye]] (or 2 [[Woad leaf|Woad leaves]], 2 [[Onion]]s 3 [[Redberries]], and 15 coins for making the dyes)." It then separately lists "*20 coins". The later GD revision 6485448 instead lists "2 [[Woad leaf|Woad leaves]] and 5 [[coins]]" for blue and "2 [[Onion]]s, 3 [[Redberries]], and 10 coins" for orange, with no separate 20-coin line. The older guide's Wyson dialogue explains the 20 coins buy the leaves; it is not evidence of an extra mandatory fee when the player already has dyes or leaves.
- The 2011 revision's rewards are "*5 [[quest point]]s", "*A [[gold bar]]", "*200 {{Skill clickpic|Crafting}} Experience"; the October 2012 revision additionally says "*2 extra spins on the [[Squeal of Fortune]]". Preserve the latter for the November 2012 target; the earlier list does not establish that spins were absent in November 2012.

## 4. Woad leaves: other sources and membership

### Leaves themselves and Wyson

Woad leaf, revision **6284190**, **2012-09-23T19:36:16Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Woad_leaf?oldid=6284190](https://runescape.wiki/w/Woad_leaf?oldid=6284190), marks the item `|members = No`, `|tradeable = Yes`, `|stackable = Yes`, `|store = 15`, `|seller = Wyson the gardener`.

Its source paragraph says: "[[Members]] can grow woad leaves at level 25 [[Farming]] by planting a [[woad seed]] in a flower patch. Alternatively, players can buy woad leaves from [[Wyson the gardener]] in [[Falador Park]]. Offering him 20 [[coins]] provides the best value, since he actually gives two for being so generous."

Thus **the leaf and Wyson purchase are free-to-play; growing it is members-only**. This source verifies Wyson as an NPC seller, but does not establish an exhaustive list of all possible sellers or drops. No other leaf-selling NPC or monster leaf drop was verified from the fetched period text.

### Farming

Woad seed, revision **6549920**, **2012-11-12T17:15:00Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Woad_seed?oldid=6549920](https://runescape.wiki/w/Woad_seed?oldid=6549920):

- Level, patch and harvest: "They are planted in a [[Flower (patch)|flower patch]] and need a farming level of 25 to be planted. When fully grown, the flower can be harvested for three [[woad leaves]]."
- Growth time and XP: `|time = 20 min (4x5 min)`, `|plant = 20.5`, `|harvest = 115.5`.
- Protection: "A nearby gardener will '''not''' watch over your growing woad plant."
- **One seed per patch**, supported by Flower (patch), revision **6446880**, **2012-10-20T11:16:59Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Flower_%28patch%29?oldid=6446880](https://runescape.wiki/w/Flower_%28patch%29?oldid=6446880): "Each patch requires '''one seed''' to begin growing that flower type."

Membership is corroborated by Farming, revision **6565110**, **2012-11-17T05:59:19Z**, [https://runescape.wiki/w/Farming?oldid=6565110](https://runescape.wiki/w/Farming?oldid=6565110): "'''Farming''' is a members' [[skill]]". The same revision states: "Most flowers yield 1 flower when picked, but [[woad leaves]] and [[limpwurt roots]] will yield 3 leaves/roots."

### Seed acquisition is not a direct leaf drop

Woad seed revision **6549920**, same source above, says "Woad seeds may be bought from the [[Vinesweeper]] [[minigame]] for 30 points each." Its dropping-monsters entry is:

> `{{ItemDropsLine|Monster=Tortoise|Combat=79, 92|Quantity=1|Rarity=Rare}}`

This is a **woad seed** drop, not a woad leaf drop. It supplies the Farming route and must not be converted into a leaf drop table entry.

No Anki process was launched and no repository content or git state was changed. Scratch fetches are confined to `/tmp/pass18-probe-d/`.

## Unverified claims

- **Wyson stock and daily limits:** no fetched pre-cutoff wiki passage explicitly says “unlimited”, gives a finite stock or specifies a daily cap. Darkan implements no limit; this remains emulator-only support for the original game.
- **Shop-window exclusivity:** the period guide explicitly establishes dialogue price selection, but does not explicitly rule out every alternate shop interface. The seller infobox's `shop=Yes` must not be used to invent a conventional stock window.
- **Dye-mixing XP, level gate and empty-vial return:** no explicit period passage was found. “No XP, no level check, one output dye and no vial” is the inspected modern Darkan behavior, not a period-wiki-proven claim. Cape dyeing's 2.5 Crafting XP must not be assigned to mixing.
- **Mixing quantities and blue/yellow or blue/red click sequence:** period text names the recipes, and the older quest guide explicitly says Use red dye with yellow dye. Exact one-of-each consumption and the generic item-on-item implementation of all three recipes are corroborated by Darkan, not explicitly enumerated in the fetched period wiki passages.
- **Other direct woad-leaf drops or NPC shops:** none verified. The fetched Woad leaf revision identifies Wyson and Farming; Goblin and Farmer period revisions did not provide a woad-leaf drop passage. This is not proof that no other source existed. Woad seed's Tortoise entry is verified only for the seed.
