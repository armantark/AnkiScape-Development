## Challenge boundary

Sumona's optional challenge replaces the ordinary roll and may be declined
without penalty. It requires defeating the Kalphite Queen while personally
dealing the majority of damage, with CoinShare/LootShare disabled. It grants
15,000 Slayer XP, 30 challenge points and one completed task, for total point
awards of 42 normally, 90 on a tenth task and 210 on a fiftieth.

The cutoff challenge is on oldid `6455602`; `Kalphite Queen` oldid `6565797`
corroborates it:
<https://runescape.wiki/w/Kalphite_Queen?oldid=6565797>.
`Talk:Sumona` oldid `4066201` records the CoinShare/LootShare failure:
<https://runescape.wiki/w/Talk:Sumona?oldid=4066201>.
Sumona oldid `5857722` corrects the fiftieth-task total from 220 to 210.

The exact challenge-offer prerequisite and probability remain
`source_blocked`. The owner-authorized five-task/10% fallback remains labeled
implementation-only. In AnkiScape's solo Combat runtime, the player necessarily
supplies all damage; no fake Social Slayer participant is created.

## Current AnkiScape readiness

This audit does **not** authorize a partial live Sumona role.

Black demon, Cave crawler, Greater demon and Hellhound are complete ordinary
Combat candidates. Banshee lacks its protection/scream/drain loop. The generic
Baby dragon collapses blue and red identity. Abyssal demon lacks its
mapless-interruption abstraction. Nechryael has an inert Death-spawn target but
no summon hook. The other 29 families have no direct target. There is no stable
Slayer family registry, assignment generator, task-credit path or playable
Smoking Kills definition, so end-to-end readiness is **0/37**.

The one safe independent data repair from this pass removes the incorrect
60 Slayer XP from Death spawn and cites oldid `6327456`. Banshee is
deliberately not patched as an ordinary magic row: its missing behavior is a
compound protection, scream, accuracy and stat-drain loop.

No owner-level design question emerged. Remaining work is source/data and
implementation: build the stable family/variant registry; add the 29 absent
families; implement the four non-certifying existing-family mechanics; make
Smoking Kills playable; then certify the full pool before exposing Sumona.
