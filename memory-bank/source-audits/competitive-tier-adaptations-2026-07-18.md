# Competitive-tier adaptations

Date: `2026-07-18`

Cutoff: RuneScape immediately before EOC, `2012-11-20`.

Status: **scope settled (all competitive activities adapt); Duel Arena
owner-settled; four pattern-determined shapes recorded below; Conquest and
Mobilising Armies have their own fork records; all period values remain
harvest work.**

## Duel Arena (owner-settled 2026-07-18)

- Live 1v1 duels against period-shaped simulated opponents via the Fist of
  Guthix bounded-opponent machinery, with authentic rule presets (no food, no
  prayer, weapon restrictions, obstacles).
- **Challenges + tournaments**: free challenge duels for the fight itself,
  plus the period Duel Tournaments (introduced pre-cutoff) with authentic
  entry fees feeding authentic prize structures — a sourced, bounded,
  self-funding coin loop.
- Staking against simulated players is excluded, with a Help entry explaining
  why: any stake paid by a simulated opponent is an invented coin faucet.

## Pattern-determined shapes (no owner fork; settled by existing contracts)

These apply the settled race pattern (Trouble Brewing), bounded-opponent
machinery (FoG), engaged-target roster (multi-enemy contract), event-scaled
budgets (Trawler), and duty beats. Their focused audits happen at harvest
time.

- **Great Orb Project** (Runecrafting-owned): per-altar rounds as duty beats
  wrangling orbs; the player's altar score races a bounded, jittered opposing
  team; authentic Runecrafting XP for essence crafted during rounds; token
  rewards through Activity Rewards.
- **Castle Wars**: a match is a score race — flag runs, barricades, and
  repairs as duty beats, defensive fights as live encounters against bounded
  raiders; tickets buy the period reward catalog.
- **Soul Wars**: soul-fragment gathering and obelisk-control pressure as duty
  beats against a bounded opposing score; team avatars resolve through the
  multi-enemy engaged-target contract; zeal XP rewards are XP purchases and
  stay hidden per packet decision 12-B.
- **Clan Wars**: the free-for-all portals become a repeatable safe-death
  skirmish context with live encounters against period-shaped simulated
  combatants; clan-vs-clan battles run as a bounded team-score race wrapping
  the same encounters.
