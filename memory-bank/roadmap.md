# AnkiScape Completion Roadmap

Status: reconciled 2026-07-16. Use `future-work-kanban.md` for actionable cards and treat `completion-audit-2026-07-10.*` as dated baseline evidence.

## Strategy

Finish by dependency layers. Seventeen complete skill surfaces, unified Combat, quests, inventory/equipment, shops, activities, and shared UI/storage foundations already exist; remaining work is three planned skills plus cross-system breadth and release closure.

## Layer 1 — Close Proven Existing-Skill Slices

- Continue `gap-backlog.json` cards only when pre-EOC evidence closes inputs, outputs, gates, values, and state transitions.
- Preserve source base values and Anki pacing separation.
- Split proven sub-slices from source-blocked families.
- Keep backend, frontend, assets, tests, source audit, and Memory Bank handoff explicit.

## Layer 2 — Grill and Build Planned Skills

- Agility and Thieving are implemented. Preserve their shared LP/failure,
  carried-risk, and cross-skill hooks as later systems integrate.
- Slayer and Summoning: Combat/acquisition-dependent systems.
- Dungeoneering: late cross-skill meta-system.

Prayer, Magic, Construction, Farming, Thieving, and Agility are implemented and must not return to this queue. Attack, Strength, Defence, Constitution, and Ranged remain partial through unified Combat rather than gaining redundant review surfaces.

## Layer 3 — Ingest Stable Darkan Evidence

Wait for a pinned high-fidelity milestone before broad ingestion, then reconcile only relevant mechanics against period evidence. Highest-value targets are planned-skill server mechanics, quest fidelity, special methods/activities, bosses/instances, reward shops, and complex equipment behavior.

## Layer 4 — Activities, Combat Contexts, and Acquisition

- Implement activities/minigames/D&Ds with authentic currencies, rewards, state, cadence, and undo.
- Complete boss/instance contexts without exposing them as ordinary farm targets.
- Close item/material routes through drops, shops, spawns, quests, claims, skills, and transformations.
- Complete specials, poison, charges/degradation, set effects, jewellery, and consumables.

## Layer 5 — Economy and Collections

- Unpark the locked fake GE only when the item universe is representative.
- Add achievement diaries, clue scrolls, collections, one-time rewards, and random/timed content.
- Keep the global review clock for world/economy cadence, not ordinary skilling minutes.

## Layer 6 — Balance and Release Closure

- Tune review pacing only after all skills and major methods exist.
- Validate long-run acquisition, item, and coin sinks.
- Run migration, real-profile Anki, performance, accessibility, packaging/license, export/recovery, and documentation passes.
- Decide personal fork versus public derivative release.

## Permanent Boundaries

- No standalone transportation network or persistent world map.
- No post-cutoff/OSRS-only substitution without independent pre-EOC proof.
- No visible inert target/reward stubs.
- No wall-clock ordinary skilling simulation.
- Do not re-grill settled designs unless new evidence creates a real product fork.
