# AnkiScape Completion Roadmap

Status: reconciled 2026-07-10. Use `memory-bank/future-work-kanban.md` for actionable cards and `memory-bank/completion-audit.json` for machine-readable coverage.

## Strategy

Finish AnkiScape by dependency layers, not by old “v1 skill” phases. The registry, review dispatch, inventory, equipment, quests, shops, activities, and UI foundations already exist. The remaining work is content breadth, missing-skill architecture, cross-system fidelity, and closure.

## Layer 1 — Close Proven Existing-Skill Slices

- Continue `gap-backlog.json` cards only when pinned pre-EOC evidence closes the concrete contract.
- Preserve source base values and Anki pacing separation.
- Split proven sub-slices from source-blocked families.
- Keep backend, frontend, assets, balance, tests, and Memory Bank handoff explicit.

## Layer 2 — Grill and Build Missing Skills

Every wholly missing skill gets a dedicated owner grill before implementation:

- Prayer: resume the existing partial grill.
- Farming and Construction: highly connected noncombat foundations.
- Magic: complete the skill beyond its live combat slice.
- Agility and Thieving: active support loops with route/loot implications.
- Slayer and Summoning: Combat-dependent systems.
- Dungeoneering: late cross-skill meta-system.

Attack, Strength, Defence, Constitution, and Ranged do not need new loop grills; they need shared Combat parity and truthful registry/status reconciliation under the existing Combat design.

## Layer 3 — Ingest Stable Darkan Restoration Evidence

The local Darkan restoration is moving. Wait for a stable milestone before broad ingestion, then diff and reconcile only the relevant areas against pinned historical evidence.

Highest-value ingestion targets:

- missing skill server mechanics;
- 194-quest fidelity and unresolved combat definitions;
- large special skilling methods and activities;
- bosses, instances, minigames, reward shops, and complex equipment behavior.

Do not wait for Darkan when literal period evidence or caches already prove the fact, and do not treat Darkan as permission to bypass the `2012-11-20` cutoff.

## Layer 4 — Activities, Combat Contexts, and Acquisition

- Grill and implement activities/minigames/D&Ds with real currencies, rewards, shops, state, clock cadence, and undo.
- Complete boss/instance contexts without exposing them as ordinary farm targets.
- Close authentic item/material acquisition routes across drops, shops, ground spawns, quests, claims, skills, and transformations.
- Complete equipment specials, poison, charges/degradation, set effects, jewellery, and consumables.

## Layer 5 — Economy and Collections

- Unpark the locked fake GE design once the skill/item universe is representative.
- Add achievement diaries, clue scrolls, collection tracking, one-time rewards, and random/timed world content.
- Keep the global review clock for world/economy cadence and resets, not ordinary skilling minutes.

## Layer 6 — Balance and Release Closure

- After all skills and major methods exist, derive ordinary-efficient 2012 guide XP/hour envelopes and tune review pacing separately.
- Close economy/acquisition loops and validate long-run item/coin sinks.
- Run migration, manual Anki, performance, accessibility, packaging/license, export/recovery, and documentation passes.
- Decide personal fork versus public derivative release.

## Permanent Boundaries

- No standalone transportation network or persistent world map/location.
- No post-cutoff/OSRS-only substitution without independent pre-EOC proof.
- No visible inert target/reward stubs.
- No wall-clock ordinary skilling simulation.
- Do not re-grill settled designs unless new evidence creates a real product fork.
