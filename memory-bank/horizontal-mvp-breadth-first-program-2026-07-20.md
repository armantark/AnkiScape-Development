# Horizontal MVP Breadth-First Program

Date: 2026-07-20

Status: **owner-directed implementation sequencing.**

This record changes implementation order, not any settled product contract,
period source rule, exclusion, or dependency boundary. The goal is to make the
whole pre-EOC product playable in breadth before repeatedly deepening already
playable families.

## What counts as a horizontal MVP

A skill or feature family reaches breadth level one only when its settled
mechanics work end to end with the smallest readily sourced data set that can
exercise them honestly. Breadth level one is **mechanic-complete, not
catalog-complete**:

1. authentic entry and unlock gates;
2. every settled state transition, policy, failure mode, and direct action in
   the feature's owning contract;
3. its real item, XP, currency, reward, or progression consequence;
4. persistence and migration where the slice adds state;
5. review/direct-action undo at every mutation boundary;
6. a usable player-facing surface with honest locks and no inert rows;
7. period-appropriate, normalized, provenance-tracked art where art is visible;
8. focused pure, integration, end-to-end, and real offscreen-Qt coverage; and
9. a source audit that separates exact evidence, labeled abstraction,
   `source_blocked` values, and deferred data depth.

A catalog entry, disabled placeholder, data table, backend-only state machine,
reward with no acquisition/use route, or representative demo that omits a
settled mechanic does not count.

The breadth pass may deliberately use only the easiest period-qualified rows:
one representative target per mechanically distinct target family, the
smallest reward set that proves acquisition and consumption, and bounded
documented parameters where a hidden roll is source-blocked. Exhaustive
rosters, full reward catalogs, rare-roll precision, conflicting historical
tables, long-tail variants, and other slow harvests belong to later data-depth
passes. Deferring data never authorizes deferring a mechanic.

## Breadth-first traversal

The implementation frontier uses these layers:

- **Level 0 — disposition:** every family is named, owned, excluded, or
  explicitly blocked.
- **Level 1 — horizontal MVP:** all settled mechanics work with a small,
  readily sourced representative data set and prove the family's shared
  substrate.
- **Level 2 — representative breadth:** add the next mechanically distinct
  data family in each feature before completing every row in one family.
- **Level 3 — parity depth:** fill the remaining easy source-closed catalog,
  reward, variant, and balance rows.
- **Level 4 — hard data harvest:** reconcile conflicts, hidden rolls, rare
  tables, and slow or brittle source recovery without redesigning the shipped
  mechanics.
- **Level 5 — release certification:** native-Anki QA, accessibility,
  packaging, migration fixtures, provenance, licensing, and release gates.

At a given level, choose the next slice by:

1. source and design readiness;
2. number of currently unrepresented families it unlocks;
3. authentic acquisition/reward reachability gained;
4. smallest bounded data set that still exercises the complete settled
   mechanic graph; then
5. period importance and player visibility.

Do not finish a deeper row merely because its research was already in flight
when a breadth-one family is ready. Read-only harvest work may be preserved,
but it does not control the frontier.

## Current frontier

The eighteen shipped skill surfaces, the unified partial-Combat stat surfaces,
Grand Exchange, Shops, Help/Details, Farming, Livid Farm, Big Chinchompa, Evil
Tree, Warriors' Guild, and the other explicitly shipped systems already satisfy
or exceed breadth level one.

The owner-settled activity wave remains the current breadth frontier:

1. Tears of Guthix;
2. Barrows;
3. Stealing Creation;
4. Shattered Heart.

Tears of Guthix is currently skipped, not removed: its exact post-refresh
reward magnitude and session beat count remain `source_blocked`.

Shooting Stars now satisfies breadth level one. It ships the complete
Mining-owned world-event state machine, layered communal depletion, first tag,
level waiting, contribution cap, persistent stardust, manual daily exchange,
temporary extra-ore effect, policies, migration, undo, Activities UI, Review
HUD, normalized period art, and focused proof. The initial-size, first-finder,
crowd, gem, and reward-rounding distributions are explicitly bounded data debt,
not missing mechanics.

Fist of Guthix, Sorceress's Garden, Penguin Hide and Seek, Vinesweeper, and
TzHaar Fight Cave now also satisfy breadth level one. Fight Caves ships the
exact persistent 63-wave roster, engaged-target Combat, unengaged pressure,
creature mechanics, Jad telegraph/healers, safe death, leave/resume, partial
and completion rewards, follower/session gates, undo, and a compact activity
surface. Vinesweeper ships the complete entry/leave, flag, Ogleroot, rabbit,
point, Farming-XP, and literal cutoff reward-shop graph without inventing a
board or wall-clock simulation. Its bounded one-roll field outcome is labeled
abstraction; exact hidden outcome rates remain later data depth. The newly
reachable Compost potion chain also has its period shop, dose, conversion,
item, price, and buy-limit seams. Tears of Guthix remains skipped until its
source-blocked post-refresh reward magnitude and session beat count are
resolved. Barrows and Shattered Heart remain data-readiness skips rather than
permission to flatten their missing combat/reward or qualifying-action
mechanics. Stealing Creation is therefore the next source- and design-ready
breadth-one frontier.

After each accepted slice, recompute the frontier from live source/runtime
evidence. Any newly discovered owner-facing fork goes into the current
implementation design-gap ledger with its blocking family and recommended
answer; source gaps remain source audits, not design questions. A difficult
data row may be deferred, but a mechanic named in the owning contract may not.
