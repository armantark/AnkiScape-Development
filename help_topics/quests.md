The Quest page is a journal and action selector. A quest can be present in the catalog while still requiring its real skills, prerequisite quests, items, routes, or Combat support.

## Starting and progressing

Search the catalog, inspect requirements, then select an available quest. Eligible reviews advance the current sourced stage. Route choices are explicit when they materially change the quest.

## Completion and rewards

Completing a quest stops Quest training and presents a clear completion point. Direct XP, items, quest points, unlocks, and structured choice rewards are granted through their live systems. Choice lamps and similar deferred rewards appear as Bank Claims.

Blocked stages explain the missing requirement rather than silently skipping it.
