Achievements summarize durable milestones across skills, Combat, quests, items, and activities.

## Progress

Difficulty tabs group related goals. Each row shows its current progress and completed state from live player data. Search is not needed here while the grouped surface remains compact.

Achievements should reward real reachable behavior. A milestone is not considered complete merely because its item or target exists in a registry.

Achievement Diaries and their equipment rewards are a larger period system; their eventual implementation must use the settled achievement architecture rather than bolting rewards onto unrelated rows.
