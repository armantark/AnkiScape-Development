AnkiScape never uses the computer's wall clock for gameplay. Closing Anki, waiting overnight, or spending longer on a card does not advance, expire, restore, or reset anything.

## What advances time

Answered reviews advance the shared simulated world clock. Both successful foreground actions and Again answers can move passive world systems because the world tick is separate from the foreground action.

## What uses it

Background growth, stock recovery, recurring eligibility, familiar duration, and other historical timers consume review-driven state according to their own contracts. Undo restores the clock and the world changes from the triggering review together.
