NPC shops are fixed vendors with their own stock, prices, services, and access rules.

## Buying and selling

Select a shop, choose Buy Items or Sell Items, then choose a quantity. Transactions use your Bank inventory and Coins. Search filters the visible catalog without changing shop state.

## Stock

Stock and price behavior follow the selected vendor. Recovery uses AnkiScape's simulated review clock; waiting in real time does nothing. The interface shows current stock and transaction prices without predicting future restocks.

Some NPC actions, such as instantaneous item services, live beside ordinary stock when they belong to that vendor.
