The Skills page is where you choose the foreground action attached to successful reviews. Categories organize skills; selecting a skill opens its targets and controls.

## Choosing training {#choosing-training}

A target must satisfy its level, quest, material, and equipment requirements before it can be selected. A grey row explains the blocker and cannot replace your current target.

**Train this skill** makes the selected target active. **Stop training** leaves reviews with passive world progress only.

## Different action families

- Gathering targets usually roll for one sourced action.
- Processing targets consume carried materials and create outputs.
- Combat keeps an encounter across reviews and uses the selected loadout.
- Utility/Activities may prepare materials, run a bounded activity beat, or manage a skill-adjacent system without pretending to be an XP skill.
