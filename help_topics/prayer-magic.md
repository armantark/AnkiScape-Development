Prayer and Magic have ordinary training targets and can also attach effects to Combat.

## Prayer

Prayer training combines a material with a method such as burial, ashes, an altar, or another source-backed route. Combat prayers and curses consume Prayer points while active. Carried passives work only when the relevant physical item is in the selected target's pack.

## Magic

Magic targets include combat and non-combat spells with their real level, rune, item, and quest gates. Combat spell selection is separate from ordinary Magic training. Time-limited spell effects use answered reviews, never wall-clock seconds.

Unavailable runes, points, materials, or unlocks leave the selected policy visible without partially consuming resources.
