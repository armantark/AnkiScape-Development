AnkiScape turns review answers into persistent RuneScape-style progress without replacing Anki's study flow.

## Start here {#start-here}

1. Open **Skills** and choose a trainable action.
2. Return to reviewing.
3. Hard, Good, and Easy answers can perform the selected action. Again still advances passive world state but does not perform the foreground action.
4. Use the floating HUD for immediate progress and the main menu for inventory, equipment, quests, and settings.

## A quiet companion

The game should never require attention while you are recalling a card. Decisions such as choosing a target, changing a loadout, or spending a reward happen in the menu. Review feedback stays compact.

Open [Review progress](help:review-progress) for answer and undo behavior, or [Skills](help:skills) to understand target selection.
