The Bank is persistent item storage and the home for direct item actions, equipment access, recovery, and reward claims.

## Finding and using items

Search matches item names and categories without changing quantities or carried-pack order. Context actions expose only operations the selected item supports, such as consume, open, equip, teleport, or discard.

**Discard X** removes the confirmed quantity instantly. Its bounded command undo is separate from review undo.

## Claims and recovery

Bank Claims collect rewards that need an explicit choice. Grave and Death's Office panels expose recoverable Combat items and costs. Target-local carried packs remain separate from Bank ownership; see [Carried inventory](help:carried-inventory).

## Tool belt

The Toolbelt panel below the Bank items shows your attached pickaxe and hatchet, then every other 2012 tool-belt tool by group. Click its header to fold it away; it stays folded until you open it again. Those tools are always with you for skills, quests, Farming and Vinesweeper, so they never need a Bank slot. The Ammo mould and Chain link mould start greyed, because a new 2012 account did not have them: right-click a greyed mould to add it, which uses up one copy from your Bank. The Ammo mould can be added only after Dwarf Cannon. A few quest steps needed a held tool in 2012, such as some quest digs that a belt spade could not do, and those still ask for one in the Bank.
