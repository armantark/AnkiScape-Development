One live familiar is a global equipment entity. The Equipment page owns that entity; skill pages and reviewer controls show synchronized projections of the same state.

## Pouches and duration

Summoning actions create pouches and scrolls from real materials. Summoning, renewal, expiry, special moves, and point restoration use review-driven state rather than the wall clock.

## Automation

A target policy may fill an empty familiar slot or replace at a safe resource boundary. It never silently dismisses a paid live familiar merely because you changed pages. Casual can choose the best available familiar in a configured role; Advanced preserves an exact selection.

Beasts of burden share the target's carried-inventory mode. Their cargo is deposited before a safe replacement.
