Collections records passive progress that related features unlock automatically.

## Music Log

The Music Log lists each sourced cutoff track with its unlock hint, provenance, and completion state. Quest, activity, enemy, and achievement milestones unlock their related tracks without audio playback.

A latent row belongs to a feature that does not yet provide durable unlock evidence. It remains visible and stays in the Completionist cutoff catalog until that feature activates it.
