The Grand Exchange is an asynchronous market simulation for tradeable items.

## Offers

Create buy or sell offers with an item, quantity, and price. Open offers occupy period-accurate offer slots and can fill over answered reviews. Collect completed items or Coins into the Bank.

## Market information

Search and filters help find registered tradeable items. The interface distinguishes current offers, completed amounts, and collection state; it does not pretend to know a guaranteed future fill.

Items marked non-tradeable use their authentic acquisition or transformation routes instead.
