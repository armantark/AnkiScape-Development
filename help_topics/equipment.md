The Equipment page owns worn gear and the single live familiar.

## Worn equipment

Each item uses its real slot, requirements, two-handed rules, and combat bonuses. Equipping or removing an item updates available Combat styles and effective stats. Items remain real Bank inventory objects rather than permanent unlock flags.

## Familiar slot

Summoning policies elsewhere project the same live familiar shown here. Manual summon, dismiss, cargo, renewal, and special controls act on that one entity. Changing a skill target does not create or replace a second familiar.

See [Combat](help:combat) for encounter loadouts and [Summoning](help:summoning) for pouch policies.
