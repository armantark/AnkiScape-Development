Combat is selected from the Skills hub but trains through one persistent encounter rather than one isolated hit per card.

## Before fighting

Choose an enemy, attack style, spell when applicable, prayer policy, food, potions, ammunition, runes, and other target-local supplies. Equipment controls available styles and combat bonuses.

## During reviews

Each eligible review advances a bounded slice of the current encounter. Kills, death, fleeing, and Bank boundaries stop that slice cleanly. Again disengages the foreground encounter for that answer while passive world effects can continue.

## Death and recovery

Death uses the shared grave and Death's Office recovery model. Items, costs, and remaining recovery state are visible from the Bank rather than hidden in a toast.

See [Equipment](help:equipment) and [Prayer and Magic](help:prayer-magic).
